Armata Pharmaceuticals Announces Financial Results for the Third Quarter 2026 and Provides Corporate Update

​

LOS ANGELES, Calif., November 12, 2026 -- Armata Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NYSE American: ARMP) (“Armata” or the “Company”), a late clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the development of high-purity and potent, pathogen-specific bacteriophage therapeutics for the treatment of antibiotic-resistant and difficult-to-treat bacterial infections, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

​

“We continue to advance toward our most important near-term objective: initiating the Phase 3 superiority study of AP-SA02,” said Armata’s Dr. Deborah Birx, Chief Executive Officer. “We have progressed from planning to establishing the operational infrastructure needed to execute a large, pivotal clinical study. Our manufacturing capabilities are well established, critical clinical trial preparations are progressing, and we continue to work closely with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”) to finalize the study design. Together, these accomplishments reflect the considerable effort and investment we have made to prepare for this important next stage of development, while maintaining fiscal discipline.”

​

“We believe the FDA’s recent Breakthrough Therapy designation further underscores AP-SA02’s clinical potential. We also appreciate the continued financial support of the U.S. Department of War (“DoW”), which comes at an important time as we prepare for this next critical stage of clinical development. We believe AP-SA02 has the potential to make a meaningful difference in patient care and, more broadly, provide a foundation for expanding our bacteriophage technology into additional indications. We are excited about the opportunity ahead and remain firmly focused on successfully executing our Phase 3 program,” concluded Dr. Birx.

​

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Developments:

​

Preparations for AP-SA02 Phase 3 Program

​

Armata continued to make significant progress in clinical, regulatory, manufacturing, and operational preparations for the planned pivotal Phase 3 superiority study of AP-SA02, the Company’s intravenously administered multi-phage product candidate for adjunct treatment of complicated Staphylococcus aureus bacteremia (“SAB”) caused by methicillin-sensitive S. aureus (“MSSA”) or methicillin-resistant S. aureus (“MRSA”).

​

●Continued discussions with the FDA to finalize the study design and submitted protocol revisions in response to FDA feedback.
●Submitted responses to all Clinical, Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (“CMC”), and Regulatory comments in the FDA’s End-of-Phase 2 meeting written response.
●Completed four engineering runs of AP-SA02 at the Company’s in-house cGMP manufacturing facility in Los Angeles, California. Production of clinical trial material for the planned Phase 3 study is underway, with one cGMP production run and vialing of AP-SA02 drug product completed in the third quarter of 2026.
●Advanced the qualification and validation of quality control release assays.
●Completed validation of critical utilities.
●Executed contracts with clinical research organizations to support study management.
●Continued negotiating clinical trial agreements with an initial group of U.S. clinical trial sites.

​

​

Collectively, these activities advance the Company’s preparations to initiate its planned pivotal Phase 3 superiority study, which is designed to support a future Biologics License Application (BLA) for AP-SA02. The Phase 3 study is expected to initiate in the second half of 2026, with exact timing depending on when FDA concurs with the protocol.

​

Regulatory

​

●Received FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation for AP-SA02 for adjunct treatment of complicated bacteremia caused by MSSA or MRSA. The designation is intended to expedite the review of medicines that treat a serious or life-threatening condition and have shown preliminary clinical evidence indicating the potential for substantial improvement over available therapies. AP-SA02 now holds Breakthrough Therapy, Fast Track and Qualified Infectious Disease Product designations, providing strong regulatory validation.
●Obtained FDA agreement on an Initial Pediatric Study Plan (“Agreed iPSP”) for future pediatric evaluation of AP-SA02 as an adjunct treatment for complicated SAB, with pediatric studies deferred pending safety and efficacy data from the planned adult Phase 3 program. We believe this plan supports potential pediatric expansion while prioritizing patient safety and efficient clinical development.

​

Funding

​

●Received $3.7 million in additional non-dilutive funding from the DoWi, the first funds received specifically for AP-SA02 Phase 3 execution under a previously announced award. This brings cumulative non-dilutive DoW funding under the award to approximately $32.4 million, including $28.7 million previously provided, underscoring continued U.S. government’s interest in advancing AP-SA02.

​

Presentations and Publications

​

●Presented new AP-SA02 data at the 20th International Symposium on Staphylococci and Staphylococcal Infections (ISSSI), held September 20–24 in Banff, Alberta, Canada. Findings suggest controlled genetic diversity may enhance killing of certain S. aureus clinical isolates by enabling individual phages to adapt to genetically and geographically diverse MRSA and MSSA isolates.
●Further advanced bacteriophage science through publication of the peer-reviewed article "Insights into Genome Ejection by a Therapeutic phiKMV-like Bacteriophage" in the Journal of Molecular Biology.
●Participated in the H.C. Wainwright 28th Annual Global Investment Conference.

​

Intellectual Property

​

●Announced the issuance of two U.S. patents covering the Company's bacteriophage programs:
oU.S. Patent No. 12,751,983, issued October 6, 2026, protects methods of treating S. aureus infections with AP-SA02, with protection until at least February 2041.
oU.S. Patent No. 12,594,312, issued April 7, 2026, protects methods of treating Pseudomonas infections with AP-PA02, with protection expected until January 2043.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

​

Grant and Award Revenue. The Company recognized grant and award revenue of $3.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $1.2 million in the comparable period in 2025. This represents the Medical Technology Enterprise Consortium's share of the costs incurred for the Company's AP-SA02 program for the treatment of SAB.

​

Research and Development. Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were approximately $6.8 million, compared to approximately $5.8 million for the comparable period in 2025.

​

General and Administrative. General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were approximately $3.7 million, compared to approximately $3.1 million for the comparable period in 2025.

​

Loss from Operations. Loss from operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $7.4 million, compared to a loss of approximately $7.8 million for the comparable period in 2025.

​

Other Income (Expense). Other income, net, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $62.2 million, compared with other expense, net, of approximately $18.9 million for the comparable period in 2025. Other income, net for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 included a gain of $69.6 million and a loss of $14.6 million, respectively, from changes in the fair value of the convertible loan.

​

Net Income (Loss). The net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $54.9 million, or $1.15 earnings per share on a basic basis and a ($0.24) loss per share on a diluted basis, as compared to a net loss of ($26.7) million, or ($0.74) loss per share on a basic and diluted basis, for the comparable period in 2025. Net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily driven by the gain of $69.6 million from the change in fair value of the convertible loan.

​

Cash and Cash Equivalents. As of September 30, 2026, Armata held approximately $18.3 million of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents, compared to $8.7 million as of December 31, 2025.

​

As of November 6, 2026, 37,192,632 common shares were outstanding.

About AP-SA02

​

Armata is developing AP-SA02, a fixed multi-phage cocktail, for the adjunct treatment of complicated Staphylococcus aureus bacteremia caused by methicillin-sensitive S. aureus (MSSA) or methicillin-resistant S. aureus (MRSA). AP-SA02 has received Qualified Infectious Disease Product (QIDP), Fast Track and Breakthrough Therapy designations from the FDA. The diSArm study (NCT05184764) was a Phase 1b/2a, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multiple ascending dose escalation study of the safety, tolerability, and efficacy of intravenous AP-SA02 in addition to best available antibiotic therapy (“BAT”) compared to BAT alone (placebo) for the treatment of adults with complicated S. aureus bacteremia. The Company highlighted positive results from the Phase 2a diSArm study in a late-breaking oral presentation at IDWeek 2025™ in October 2025. The Company plans to advance AP-SA02 into a Phase 3 superiority study in complicated SAB, anticipated to initiate in the second half of 2026, with exact timing depending on when FDA concurs with the protocol.

About Armata Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

​

Armata is a late clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the development of high-purity and potency, pathogen-specific bacteriophage therapeutics for the treatment of antibiotic-resistant and difficult-to-treat bacterial infections using its proprietary bacteriophage-based technology. Armata is developing and advancing a broad pipeline of natural and synthetic phage candidates, including clinical candidates for Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Staphylococcus aureus, and other important pathogens. Armata is committed to advancing phage therapy with drug development expertise that spans bench to clinic, including in-house phage-specific current Good Manufacturing Practices (“cGMP”) manufacturing to support full commercialization.
