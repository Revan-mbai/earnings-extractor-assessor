Modine Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results

​

Strong growth in Data Centers and Commercial HVAC segments supports higher full-year revenue outlook for core business following the spin-off of Performance Technologies

Racine, WI – November 3, 2026 – Modine (NYSE: MOD), a diversified global leader in thermal management technology and solutions, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Second Quarter Highlights:

●Net sales of $994.4 million increased $255.5 million, or 35 percent, from the prior year
●Net earnings of $30.3 million decreased $14.5 million, or 32 percent, from the prior year, including $29.5 million of income tax expense related to legal entity restructuring to facilitate the spin-off of the Performance Technologies business
●Adjusted EBITDA of $136.2 million increased $32.4 million, or 31 percent, from the prior year
●Earnings per share of $0.56 decreased $0.27, or 33 percent, from the prior year
●Adjusted earnings per share of $1.35 increased $0.29, or 27 percent, from the prior year
●Completed the spin-off of Performance Technologies on October 1

​

“Data Centers and Commercial HVAC delivered strong quarters, with revenue growth of 95% and 23%, respectively,” said Modine President and Chief Executive Officer, Neil D. Brinker. “Demand for our products remains very robust, highlighted by record orders and backlog in our Data Centers segment and expanding opportunities to serve the data center cooling market within Commercial HVAC. We have bolstered our supply chain by entering into long-term agreements with multiple strategic suppliers to secure critical components to meet our existing backlog and future business needs. We are confident in our higher revenue outlook for our core business following the successful spin-off of Performance Technologies and remain well-equipped to drive long-term sustainable growth.”

Second Quarter Financial Results

Net sales increased 35 percent to $994.4 million, compared with $738.9 million in the prior year. Sales growth was driven by higher sales in the Data Centers and Commercial HVAC segments.

Gross profit increased 25 percent to $205.4 million and gross margin decreased by 160 basis points to 20.7 percent. Gross profit increased in the Data Centers and Commercial HVAC segments, while Performance Technologies experienced a slight decline in gross profit. The decrease in gross margin resulted from lower gross margins in each of the three business segments, as further discussed in the segment review below.

Selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses increased 24 percent to $104.6 million, but decreased as a percentage of sales. The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily due to higher expenses in the Data Centers segment to support growth and costs related to the spin-off of the Performance Technologies business.

Operating income increased 33 percent to $97.8 million. The increase was driven by higher gross profit on higher sales volume, as compared to the prior year, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses to support growth and to prepare for the spin-off of the Performance Technologies business. The Company recorded $3.0 million of restructuring expenses during the quarter, primarily severance expenses related to headcount reductions and costs related to equipment transfers. In addition, the Company incurred $14.1 million in costs related to the spin-off of the Performance Technologies business. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes restructuring expenses, disposition costs, interest expense, the provision for income taxes, and depreciation and amortization expense, was $136.2 million, an increase of $32.4 million, or 31 percent compared to the prior year.

Earnings per share was $0.56, compared with $0.83 in the prior year, a decrease of $0.27 or 33 percent. This decrease was due to a higher provision for income taxes, driven by the income tax impacts of legal entity restructuring steps completed to facilitate the spin-off of the Performance Technologies business. Adjusted earnings per share was $1.35, compared with adjusted earnings per share of $1.06 in the prior year, an increase of $0.29 or 27 percent.

Second Quarter Segment Review

●Data Centers segment sales were $439.7 million, compared with $225.0 million one year ago, an increase of 95 percent. This increase was primarily driven by higher sales to hyperscale customers in North America. The segment reported gross margin of 19.7 percent, which was 470 basis points lower than the prior year. This decrease was primarily due to higher material, component and tariff costs. SG&A expenses decreased as a percentage of sales due to the significant increase in revenue. The segment reported operating income of $61.2 million, a 70 percent increase from the prior year, and adjusted EBITDA of $67.6 million, a 66 percent increase from the prior year.
●Commercial HVAC segment sales were $285.5 million, compared with $232.9 million one year ago, an increase of 23 percent. This increase was primarily driven by higher sales of coils, coatings, and heating and ventilation products. The segment reported gross margin of 23.1 percent, which was 140 basis points lower than the prior year, primarily due to unfavorable sales mix and higher material and tariff costs. The segment reported operating income of $34.3 million, a 30 percent increase from the prior year, and adjusted EBITDA of $43.0 million, a 22 percent increase from the prior year.
●Performance Technologies segment sales were $287.7 million, compared with $286.3 million one year ago, an increase of less than 1 percent. Higher sales to power generation and commercial vehicle customers were largely offset by lower sales to automotive and off highway customers. The segment reported gross margin of 18.6 percent, which was 30 basis points lower than the prior year, primarily due to higher material and tariff costs. The segment reported operating income of $32.1 million, an 8 percent increase from the prior year, and adjusted EBITDA of $40.9 million, a 3 percent decrease from the prior year.

Balance Sheet & Liquidity

Net cash used for operating activities for the six months ended September 30, 2026, was $57.1 million, a decrease of $86.2 million compared to the prior year. Free cash flow for the six months ended September 30, 2026, was a use of $145.6 million, a decrease of $115.3 million from the prior year. This decrease was primarily due to higher capital expenditures to increase production capacity in the Data Centers segment, and higher net working capital including increased inventory levels to support growing customer demand for data center cooling products. Cash payments for restructuring activities, disposition costs and certain other items totaled $27.7 million during the six months ended September 30, 2026.

Total debt was $724.3 million as of September 30, 2026. Cash and cash equivalents totaled $150.6 million as of September 30, 2026. Net debt was $573.7 million as of September 30, 2026, an increase of $210.9 million from the end of fiscal 2026. This increase primarily resulted from increases in working capital and purchases of stock in conjunction with our equity compensation plan. Under this plan, participants have the option to sell back shares of their vested equity awards to satisfy individual tax withholding obligations. These repurchased shares are held as treasury stock, which reduces the number of shares outstanding used to calculate earnings per share.

Outlook

“We are updating our Fiscal 2027 financial outlook to reflect the higher expectations for our core businesses and the successful spin-off of the Performance Technologies business,” said Modine President and Chief Executive Officer, Neil D. Brinker. “Starting in the third fiscal quarter, the historical results for Performance Technologies will be reported as discontinued operations, and we have revised our continuing outlook accordingly. We expect revenues to increase 50 to 55 percent from the prior year, excluding Performance Technologies from both periods. Additionally, we expect full-year adjusted EBITDA for our continuing business to be $470 to $500 million, representing a growth rate in excess of 50 percent from the prior year, excluding Performance Technologies from both periods. In addition, I am looking forward to sharing our longer term revenue and earnings targets during our upcoming Analyst and Investor Day on November 18.”

Non-GAAP Financial Disclosures

Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted earnings per share, net debt, free cash flow, organic sales and organic sales growth (which are defined below) as used in this press release are not measures that are defined in generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP). These non-GAAP measures are used by management as performance measures to evaluate the Company’s overall financial performance and liquidity. These measures are not, and should not be viewed as, substitutes for the applicable GAAP measures, and may be different from similarly titled measures used by other companies.

Definition – Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin

The Company defines adjusted EBITDA as net earnings excluding interest expense, the provision or benefit for income taxes, depreciation and amortization expenses, other income and expense, restructuring expenses, impairment charges, pension termination charges, acquisition and disposition costs, and certain other gains or charges. Adjusted EBITDA margin represents adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of net sales. The Company believes that adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin provide relevant measures of profitability and earnings power. The Company views these financial metrics as being useful in assessing operating performance from period to period by excluding certain items that it believes are not representative of its core business. Adjusted EBITDA, when calculated for the business segments, is defined as operating income excluding depreciation and amortization expenses, restructuring expenses, impairment charges, and certain other gains or charges.

Definition – Adjusted earnings per share

Diluted earnings per share plus restructuring expenses, impairment charges, pension termination charges, acquisition and disposition costs, and excluding changes in income tax valuation allowances and certain other gains or charges. Adjusted earnings per share is an overall performance measure, not including costs associated with restructuring, acquisitions, and dispositions, and certain other gains or charges.

Definition – Net debt

The sum of debt due within one year and long-term debt, less cash and cash equivalents. Net debt is an indicator of the Company's debt position after considering on-hand cash balances.

Definition – Free cash flow

Free cash flow represents net cash provided by operating activities less expenditures for property, plant and equipment. Free cash flow presents cash generated from operations during the period that is available for strategic capital decisions.

Definition – Organic sales and organic sales growth

Net sales and net sales growth can be impacted by acquisitions, dispositions, and foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations. The Company defines organic sales as external net sales excluding the impact of acquisitions and the effects of foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations. Organic sales growth represents the percentage change of organic sales compared to prior year external net sales, excluding the impact of dispositions. The effect of exchange rate changes is calculated by using the same foreign currency exchange rates as those used to translate financial data for the prior period. The Company adjusts for acquisitions and dispositions by excluding net sales in the current and prior periods, respectively, for which there are no comparable sales in the reported periods. These sales growth measures provide a more consistent indication of our performance, without the effects of foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations or acquisitions and dispositions.

Forward-looking non-GAAP financial measure

 

The Company’s fiscal 2027 guidance excludes the results of the Performance Technologies business, which will be reported as a discontinued operation starting for the third quarter of fiscal 2027. Adjusted EBITDA, as defined above, is a non-GAAP financial measure. The fiscal 2027 guidance includes the following estimates for the Company’s remaining businesses (excluding Performance Technologies): interest expense of approximately $26 to $30 million; a provision for income taxes of approximately $95 to $105 million; and depreciation and amortization expense of approximately $56 to $61 million. The non-GAAP financial measure also excludes certain cash and non-cash expenses or gains. These expenses and gains may be significant and include items such as restructuring expenses (including severance and equipment transfer costs), impairment charges, acquisition and disposition costs, and certain other items. These expenses for the first six months of fiscal 2027 are presented on page 9. In connection with the Reverse Morris Trust transaction with Gentherm, the Company expects to incur approximately $20 to $25 million of additional costs during the second half of fiscal 2027, primarily for transaction advisory, legal, accounting, tax and other professional services. Estimates of other expenses and gains for the remainder of fiscal 2027 are not available due to the low visibility and unpredictability of these items.
