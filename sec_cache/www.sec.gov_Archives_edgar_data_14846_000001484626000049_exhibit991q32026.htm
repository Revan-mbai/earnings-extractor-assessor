Third Quarter 2026 and Subsequent Highlights
•Reported net loss per diluted share of $3.9 million or $0.23 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $2.7 million or $0.14 in the corresponding period in the prior year.
•Funds from Operations, or FFO, of $0.26 per diluted share, compared to $0.28 in the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted Funds from Operations, or AFFO, of $0.31 per diluted share, compared to $0.36 in the third quarter of 2025.
•Equity in loss of unconsolidated joint ventures was a loss of $411,000 compared to a $75,000 loss in the third quarter of 2025.
•Combined Portfolio NOI was $15.1 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $15.3 million for the corresponding prior-year period.
•On June 2, 2026, the Company entered into an agreement to acquire Ranch Lake Apartments, a 336-unit multi-family property located in Bradenton, FL. The Company is to acquire the property for approximately $80 million (subject to customary closing purchase price adjustments), including the assumption of an approximate $45.7 million mortgage insured by HUD. The mortgage carries an interest rate of 2.91% and matures in 2056. The closing is anticipated to take place during the first quarter of 2027.
•On July 30, 2026, the Company refinanced the maturing mortgage of $27.8 million (bearing an interest rate of 3.73%) on Civic Center 2 - Southaven, MS with a new mortgage of $47.9 million; such new mortgage matures on August 1, 2036, bears an interest rate of 5.38% and is interest only through maturity.
•On August 17, 2026, a joint venture in which the Company has a 70% interest, acquired Riverbend Apartments, a 192-unit multi-family property located in League City, TX for $32.7 million, including a $23.5 million mortgage. The mortgage matures in 2033, bears a 5.47% fixed interest rate, and is interest only through maturity. The Company contributed $8.68 million to the joint venture for its equity interest and working capital purposes.
•On August 17, 2026, the Company paid off the $7.7 million HUD insured mortgage, maturing in 2045, bearing a fixed interest rate of 3.65% and secured by Newbridge Commons - Columbus, OH. No prepayment penalty was incurred upon payment.
•On August 25, 2026, the unconsolidated joint venture that owns The Pointe at Lenox Park-Atlanta, GA property refinanced the maturing mortgage of $23.8 million (bearing an interest rate of 3.97%) on such with a new mortgage of $26.1 million; such new mortgage matures on September 1, 2036, bears a fixed interest rate of 5.45% and is interest only through maturity.
•On September 17, 2026, the Company's $40 million credit facility, maturing in September 2027, was amended to reduce the interest rate to one-month SOFR plus 210 basis points, reduce the minimum interest rate payable to 5% and reduce the adjustable cap rate in determining the value of the assets security the facility to 6.25%. At November 2, 2026, $40 million was available under the facility.

Contemplated Acquisition

On June 2, 2026, the Company entered into an agreement to acquire Ranch Lake Apartments, a 336-unit multi-family property located in Bradenton, Florida. The purchase price is approximately $80,000,000 (subject to customary closing purchase price adjustments), including the assumption of an approximately $45,700,000 mortgage insured by the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development ("HUD"). The mortgage carries an interest rate of 2.91% and matures in 2056.

The completion of the transaction, which is anticipated to close in the first quarter of 2027, is subject to, among other things, HUD and lender approval of the mortgage assumption, and other customary closing conditions.

Completed Acquisition
On August 17, 2026, a joint venture in which the Company has a 70% interest, acquired Riverbend Apartments, a 192 unit multi-family property located in League City, TX (the "Texas Acquisition").The venture acquired the property for $32,700,000 (including a $23,450,000 mortgage). The mortgage matures in 2033, bears a 5.47% fixed interest rate (after a credit spread buydown for which the joint venture paid $469,000), and is interest only through maturity. The Company contributed $8,575,000 to the joint venture for its equity interest.

Refinancings
On July 30, 2026, the Company refinanced the maturing mortgage of $27,767,000 and bearing an interest rate of 3.73% on Civic Center 2 - Southaven, MS with a new mortgage of $47,864,000; such new mortgage matures on August 1, 2036, bears a fixed interest rate of 5.38% (after a credit spread buy down for which the Company paid $957,000) and is interest only through maturity.
On August 25, 2026, the joint venture in which the Company has a 74% interest, refinanced the maturing mortgage of $23,794,000 (bearing an interest rate of 3.97%) on The Pointe at Lenox Park-Atlanta, GA with a new mortgage of $26,057,000; such new mortgage matures on September 1, 2036, bears an interest rate of 5.45% (after a credit spread buy down for which the joint venture paid $521,000), and is interest only for the term of the mortgage.
Mortgage Payoff
In August 2026, the Company paid off the $7,753,000 HUD insured mortgage, maturing in 2045, bearing a fixed interest rate of 3.65% and secured by Newbridge Commons, Columbus, OH. No prepayment penalty was incurred upon payment.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL measures, definitions, and reconciliations

Adjusted Funds from Operations (AFFO)
BRT computes AFFO by adjusting FFO for loss on extinguishment of debt, our straight-line rent and rent concession accruals, restricted stock and RSU compensation expense, fair value adjustment of mortgage debt, gain on insurance recovery, insurance recovery from casualty loss and deferred mortgage and debt costs (including, in each case as applicable, from its share of its unconsolidated joint ventures). Since the NAREIT White Paper(as described below) does not provide guidelines for computing AFFO, the computation of AFFO may vary from one REIT to another.

Combined Portfolio
Combined portfolio refers to the consolidated same store properties, the unconsolidated same store properties presented on a pro rata share basis.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio
Debt service coverage ratio is net operating income ("NOI") divided by total debt service and includes both consolidated and unconsolidated assets.

Funds from Operations (FFO)
BRT computes FFO in accordance with the “White Paper on Funds from Operations” issued by the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts (“NAREIT”) and NAREIT's related guidance. FFO is defined in the White Paper as net income (calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles).

Net Operating Income (NOI)
NOI is defined as "Rental and other revenue from real estate properties" less "Real estate operating expenses" in each case as presented on our statements of operations. Real estate operating expenses include real estate taxes, insurance, property management expense, utilities, repairs and maintenance, administrative and marketing. Other REIT’s may use different methodologies for calculating NOI, and accordingly, BRT's NOI may not be comparable to other REIT’s. The Company believes NOI provides an operating perspective not immediately apparent from GAAP operating income or net income (loss). NOI is one of the measures BRT uses to evaluate its performance because it (i) measures the core operations of property performance by excluding corporate level expenses and other items unrelated to property operating performance and (ii) captures trends in rental housing and property operating expenses. However, NOI should only be used as an alternative measure of BRT's financial performance.

Pro-Rata Share
BRT's pro-rata share gives effect to its percentage equity interest in the unconsolidated joint ventures that own properties. Due to the operation of allocation/distribution provision of the joint venture agreements pursuant to which BRT participates in the ownership of these properties, BRT's share of the gain and loss on the sale of a property may be less than implied by BRT's percentage equity interest. Notwithstanding the foregoing, when referring to the number of units, average occupancy, and average rent per unit, the amount shown reflects 100% of the amount.

Same Store
Same store properties refer to stabilized properties (as described below) that are owned and operated for the entirety of periods being compared, except for properties that are under construction, legacy assets, in lease-up, or are undergoing development or redevelopment. We move properties previously excluded from our same store portfolio (because they were under construction, in lease up or are in development or redevelopment) into the same store designation once they have stabilized and such status has been reflected fully in all applicable periods of comparison.

Stabilized Properties
Newly constructed, lease-up, development and redevelopment properties are deemed stabilized upon the earlier to occur of the first full calendar quarter beginning (a) 12 months after the property is fully completed and put in service and (b) attainment of at least 90% physical occupancy. 

Total Debt Service
Total debt service is the cash required to cover the repayment of interest and principal on a debt for a particular period. Total debt service is used in the calculation of the debt service coverage ratio which is used to determine the borrower’s ability to make debt service payments.
