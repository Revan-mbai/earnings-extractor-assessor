Freshpet, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Delivers 10% Net Sales Growth
Achieves Gross Margin of 45% and Adjusted Gross Margin of 50%
Raises and Narrows 2026 Outlook
Bedminster, N.J. – November 4, 2026 – Freshpet, Inc. (“Freshpet” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: FRPT) today reported financial results for its third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights Compared to Prior Year Period
•Net sales of $317.8 million, an increase of 10.0%.
•Gross margin of 44.5%, compared to the prior year period of 39.5%.
•Adjusted Gross Margin of 50.0%, compared to the prior year period of 46.0%.1
•Net income of $29.7 million, compared to the prior year period net income of $101.7 million, which included a discrete $77.9 million tax benefit as a result of sustained profitability.
•Adjusted EBITDA of $64.5 million, or 20.3% as a percentage of net sales, compared to the prior year period of $54.6 million, or 18.9% as a percentage of net sales.1
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the strength and durability of the Freshpet business model. In a challenging consumer and intense competitive environment, we delivered double-digit net sales growth, expanded margins and achieved our strongest Adjusted Gross Margin in more than seven years, while continuing to grow market share,” commented Billy Cyr, Freshpet’s Chief Executive Officer. “We are executing well against the priorities within our control and strengthening the competitive advantages that differentiate Freshpet, including the quality of our products, our manufacturing scale and expertise, our expanding omnichannel presence and our growing franchise of highly loyal pet parents. We believe these advantages provide a strong foundation to deliver sustainable, profitable growth, capture a meaningful share of the long-term opportunity in fresh pet food and fulfill our mission to help pets live longer, happier and healthier lives with the people who love them.”
Third Quarter 2026
Net sales increased 10.0% to $317.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $288.8 million in the prior year period. The increase in net sales was primarily driven by volume gains of 10.0%.
Gross profit was $141.3 million, or 44.5% as a percentage of net sales, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $114.2 million, or 39.5% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period. Gross profit as a percentage of net sales increased primarily due to improved leverage on plant expenses, reduced quality costs and lower input costs. For the third quarter of 2026, Adjusted Gross Profit was $158.8 million, or 50.0% as a percentage of net sales, compared to $132.8 million, or 46.0% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period.1
Selling, general and administrative expenses (“SG&A”) were $106.2 million, or 33.4% as a percentage of net sales, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $89.3 million, or 30.9% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period. SG&A as a percentage of net sales increased primarily due to increased logistics costs and variable compensation accrual, partially offset by decreased media spend as a percentage of net sales. Adjusted SG&A for the third quarter of 2026 was $94.2 million, or 29.6% as a percentage of net sales, compared to $78.2 million, or 27.1% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period.1

Net income was $29.7 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $101.7 million in the prior year period. The decrease in net income was due to the $77.9 million deferred income tax benefit recognized in the prior year period as a result of the release of the majority of the valuation allowance, compared to income tax expense in the current year period, and increases in SG&A, partially offset by contributions from higher sales.
Adjusted EBITDA was $64.5 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $54.6 million in the prior year period. The increase in Adjusted EBITDA was a result of increased Adjusted Gross Profit, partially offset by higher Adjusted SG&A.
First Nine Months of 2026
Net sales increased 12.8% to $921.1 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $816.8 million in the prior year period. The increase in net sales was primarily driven by volume gains of 13.3%, partially offset by unfavorable price/mix of 0.5%.
Gross profit was $390.7 million, or 42.4% as a percentage of net sales, for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $326.2 million, or 39.9% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period. Gross profit as a percentage of net sales increased primarily due to improved leverage on plant expenses and lower input costs. For the first nine months of 2026, Adjusted Gross Profit was $446.8 million, or 48.5% as a percentage of net sales, compared to $377.1 million, or 46.2% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period.1
SG&A were $329.5 million, or 35.8% as a percentage of net sales, for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $295.0 million, or 36.1% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period. SG&A as a percentage of net sales decreased primarily due to a decrease in non-recurring charges compared to 2025, partially offset by increased logistics costs and variable compensation accrual. Adjusted SG&A for the first nine months of 2026 was $291.9 million, or 31.7% as a percentage of net sales, compared to $242.5 million, or 29.7% as a percentage of net sales, in the prior year period.
Net income was $97.7 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $105.3 million in the prior year period. The decrease in net income was due to the $77.9 million deferred income tax benefit recognized in the prior year period, compared to income tax expense in the current year period, and increased logistics costs and variable compensation accrual, partially offset by the $66.6 million gain on equity investment, contributions from higher sales, and decreased non-recurring SG&A charges.
Adjusted EBITDA was $154.6 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $134.5 million in the prior year period. The increase in Adjusted EBITDA was a result of increased Adjusted Gross Profit, partially offset by higher Adjusted SG&A.
Balance Sheet
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $345.9 million with $399.0 million of debt outstanding, net of $3.5 million of unamortized debt issuance costs. Cash and cash equivalents increased $67.9 million compared to $278.0 million as of December 31, 2025, primarily as a result of the $100.0 million of cash proceeds received from the sale of our equity investment and $65.3 million of Free Cash Flow, partially offset by $102.3 million of share repurchases pursuant to the previously announced share repurchase program. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, cash from operations was $145.5 million, an increase of $40.1 million compared to the prior year period.
Outlook
For full year 2026, the Company is updating its guidance and now expects the following:
•Net sales growth in the range of 12% to 13%, compared to growth of 10% to 12% in the previous guidance;
•Adjusted EBITDA in the range of $215 million to $220 million, compared to $210 million to $220 million in the previous guidance; and
•Positive Free Cash Flow with capital expenditures between $125 to $150 million, compared to ~$150 million in the previous guidance.
The Company is reiterating its long-term guidance. For full year 2027, the Company expects:
•Net sales well in excess of the category growth rate, unchanged;
•Adjusted Gross Margin of at least 49%, unchanged; and
•Adjusted EBITDA margin in the range of 20% to 22%, unchanged.

About Freshpet
Freshpet's mission is to help dogs and cats live longer, happier, healthier lives with the people who love them. Developed by on-staff Veterinary Nutritionists, Veterinarians and Food Scientists, recipes are made from whole ingredients, like fresh meats, vegetables and fruits, and are cooked in small batches at lower temperatures to preserve their natural goodness and made at our Freshpet Kitchens. Freshpet foods and treats are kept refrigerated until they arrive at Freshpet Fridges in local markets or delivered directly to consumers.
Freshpet is available in select grocery, mass, digital, pet specialty, and club retailers across the United States, Canada and Europe, as well as online in the U.S. From the care they take to source their ingredients and make their food, to the moment it reaches your home, Freshpet's commitment to integrity, transparency and social responsibility is a point of pride.

Adjusted Gross Profit: Freshpet defines Adjusted Gross Profit as gross profit before depreciation expense, non-cash share-based compensation and loss on disposal of manufacturing equipment.
Adjusted SG&A Expenses: Freshpet defines Adjusted SG&A as SG&A expenses before depreciation and amortization expense, non-cash share-based compensation, loss on disposal of equipment, distributor transition costs, legal obligation and international business charges.
EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA: EBITDA represents net income plus depreciation and amortization expense, interest expense net of interest income and income tax expense (benefit), and Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA less gain on equity investment, plus non-cash share-based compensation expense, loss on disposal of property, plant and equipment, distributor transition costs, legal obligation, and international business charges.
Free Cash Flow: Freshpet defines Free Cash Flow as net cash flows provided by operating activities less capital expenditures.
Management believes that the non-GAAP financial measures are meaningful to investors because they provide a view of the Company with respect to ongoing operating results. The non-GAAP measures are not and should not be considered an alternative to the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures or any other figure calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP, or as an indicator of operating performance.
