MGP Ingredients Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Company reaffirms full-year 2026 guidance, declares $0.12 quarterly dividend and will become Sempreon, Inc. (Nasdaq: CASK) on November 2
ATCHISON, Kan., October 28, 2026 - MGP Ingredients, Inc. (Nasdaq: MGPI), a leading provider of branded and distilled spirits and food ingredient solutions, which will change its name to Sempreon, Inc. and its ticker symbol to CASK on November 2, 2026, today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. During the quarter, the company continued to execute against its strategic roadmap, reducing total debt by $28.4 million and delivering adjusted EBITDA and adjusted basic EPS ahead of its expectations.

“Our third quarter results show that we are doing what we said we would do. Our cash flow from operations was $32 million during the quarter, and adjusted EBITDA, adjusted basic EPS and ending net leverage each came in better than our expectations. As a result, we are reaffirming guidance,” said Julie Francis, president and CEO. “In Branded Spirits, Penelope Bourbon sales grew 12% and our mid-priced brands grew 6%. Distilling Solutions continued to navigate an oversupplied market and Ingredient Solutions grew sales by 5%. As we become Sempreon on November 2, our focus remains on executing our strategic roadmap in each of our three businesses, supported by our financial discipline and our strong right to win.”

Consolidated Results
Third quarter 2026 versus prior year
–Sales decreased by 10% to $117.9 million, primarily due to expected lower brown goods sales in Distilling Solutions and lower sales of international private label bottled products within the Branded Spirits’ other category, partially offset by higher Ingredient Solutions sales.
–Gross profit decreased 18% to $40.5 million and gross margin declined approximately 350 basis points to 34.3%, as higher waste starch stream costs in Ingredient Solutions, higher costs in Branded Spirits and lower brown goods volume more than offset lower distillation costs in Distilling Solutions.
–Operating income decreased 43% to $12.0 million. On an adjusted basis, operating income decreased by 44% to $14.0 million.
–Adjusted EBITDA decreased 33% to $21.5 million.
–Advertising and promotion expenses decreased 4% to $6.4 million, reflecting the timing of spend

throughout the year as well as savings resulting from outside agency consolidation.
–Selling, general and administrative expense increased 14%, primarily due to a higher incentive compensation accrual, partially offset by cost savings initiatives. Adjusted SG&A increased 13% and represented 17% of consolidated sales.
–Net income was $6.9 million and basic EPS was $0.32. On an adjusted basis, net income and basic EPS were $8.6 million and $0.39 per share, respectively.
–Year-to-date operating cash outflows were $8.9 million, inclusive of $48.7 million of the Penelope earnout payment classified in operating activities; excluding that payment, year-to-date operating cash inflows were $39.8 million. The net debt leverage ratio was approximately 3.6x at September 30, 2026.

Branded Spirits
Third quarter 2026 versus prior year
–Sales of $58.2 million decreased 4% versus $60.7 million.
–Gross profit of $29.2 million decreased 9% versus $32.2 million.
–Gross margin of 50.2% decreased by 280 basis points versus 53.0%.

Key developments versus prior year
–Excluding the other products category, which consists primarily of international sales of private label bottled products, sales were in line with the prior year and were negatively impacted by distributor system cutovers.
–Premium plus sales decreased by 1%. Penelope Bourbon sales increased 12%.
–Combined sales of the mid- and value-priced portfolios improved slightly, with mid-priced offerings growing 6%, led by Ezra Brooks 90 Proof, Exotico tequila and Juarez tequila.

Distilling Solutions
Third quarter 2026 versus prior year
–Sales of $29.0 million decreased 29% versus $40.9 million.
–Gross profit of $9.4 million decreased 34% versus $14.2 million.
–Gross margin of 32.4% decreased by 230 basis points versus 34.7%.

Key developments versus prior year
–As expected, elevated industry-wide barrel inventories resulted in a 47% decline in brown goods sales.
–Sales of white goods and other co-products increased 32% primarily due to higher sales volumes.
–Warehouse services revenue increased 3%.
–Higher white goods production volumes and cost savings initiatives led to lower distillation costs, partially offsetting the impact of lower brown goods sales.

Ingredient Solutions
Third quarter 2026 versus prior year
–Sales of $30.8 million increased 5% versus $29.3 million.
–Gross profit of $1.8 million decreased 39% versus $3.0 million.
–Gross margin of 6.0% decreased 430 basis points versus 10.3%.

Key developments versus prior year
–Sales growth was driven by higher sales of specialty wheat proteins, including textured proteins, and biofuel and other byproducts, partially offset by lower specialty wheat starch sales volumes primarily due to longer-than-expected downtime following a scheduled plant shutdown.
–Higher waste starch stream costs pressured profitability, partially offset by an initial insurance recovery payment related to a 2025 equipment outage.

About MGP Ingredients, Inc.
MGP Ingredients Inc. (Nasdaq: MGPI) has been formulating excellence since 1941 by bringing product ideas to life across the alcoholic beverage and specialty ingredient industries through three segments: Branded Spirits, Distilling Solutions and Ingredient Solutions. MGPI is one of the leading spirits distillers with an award-winning portfolio of premium brands including Penelope, Rebel, Remus and Yellowstone bourbons and El Mayor tequila, under the Luxco umbrella. With distilleries in Indiana and Kentucky; a tequila distillery in Arandas, Mexico; and bottling operations in Missouri, Ohio and Northern Ireland, the company creates distilled spirits for customers including many world-renowned spirits brands. In addition, the company’s high-quality specialty fiber, protein and starch ingredients provide functional, nutritional and sensory solutions for a wide range of food products. On November 2, 2026, MGP Ingredients, Inc. will become Sempreon, Inc. To learn more visit MGPIngredients.com.

DESCRIPTION OF NON-GAAP ITEMS

(a)MGP Earnings is defined as "Net income used in Earnings Per Common Share calculation," which accounts for the impacts of the earnings attributable to noncontrolling interest and earnings attributable to participating securities.

(b)Impairment and other relates to the first quarter of 2026 write-down of goodwill, indefinite-lived intangible assets and other long-lived assets as well as expenses incurred related to the idled Kentucky facilities. It is included in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss) as a component of operating income and relates to the Branded Spirits segment.

(c)The executive transition costs are included in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss) within the selling, general, and administrative line item. The adjustment includes costs related to the transition of certain executive and board of director positions.

(d)The professional services fees are included in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss) within the selling, general, and administrative line item. The adjustment includes costs related to professional services in conjunction with the goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible assets impairment valuation.

(e)The restructuring and other costs are included in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss) within the selling, general, and administrative line item. The adjustment includes special one-time severance costs related to the reduction in force that occurred during the period and other special project costs related to the corporate name change.

(f)Income tax items are included in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss) within the income tax expenses. The adjustment relates to the impacts of state law changes on the Company’s deferred tax balances.

(g)Fair value of contingent consideration relates to the quarterly adjustment of the contingent consideration liability related to the acquisition of Penelope Bourbon LLC. It is included in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss) as a component of operating income and relates to the Branded Spirits segment.
