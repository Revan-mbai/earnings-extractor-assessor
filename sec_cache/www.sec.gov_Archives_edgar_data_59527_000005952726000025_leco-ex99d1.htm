LINCOLN ELECTRIC REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

​

​

​

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

​

◾
Net sales increase 15.1% to $1,221 million; organic sales increase 14.5% led by volume growth
◾
Operating income margin increases to 17.3% and adjusted operating income margin increases to 18.0%
◾
EPS increases 19% to $2.63 and adjusted EPS increases 21% to $2.98
◾
Cash flows from operations of $216 million; 107% cash conversion
◾
Returned $143 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases

​

​

CLEVELAND, Tuesday, October 27, 2026 - Lincoln Electric Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: LECO) today reported third quarter 2026 net income of $144.3 million, or diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $2.63, which includes special item after-tax net charges of $19.3 million, or $0.35 EPS. This compares with prior year period net income of $122.6 million, or $2.21 EPS, which included special item after-tax net charges of $14.6 million, or $0.26 EPS. Excluding special items, third quarter 2026 adjusted net income was $163.6 million, or $2.98 adjusted EPS. This compares with adjusted net income of $137.2 million, or $2.47 adjusted EPS, in the prior year period.

​

Third quarter 2026 sales increased 15.1% to $1,221.3 million reflecting a 14.5% increase in organic sales, a 0.5% benefit from acquisitions and a 0.1% favorable foreign exchange. Operating income for the third quarter 2026 was $211.4 million, or 17.3% of sales. This compares with operating income of $176.7 million, or 16.6% of sales, in the prior year period. Excluding special items, adjusted operating income was $220.0 million, or 18.0% of sales, as compared with $184.6 million, or 17.4% of sales, in the prior year period.

​

“We achieved strong third quarter results led by higher volumes, an increase in our adjusted operating income margin, and record adjusted earnings per share,” said Steven B. Hedlund, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “Our performance underscores the agility of our global team who are focused on supporting customers, diligently managing inflationary headwinds, and accelerating RISE strategy initiatives and investments to optimize long-term value for all our stakeholders.”

​

Nine Month 2026 Summary

​

Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $439.2 million, or $7.97 EPS, which includes special item after-tax net charges of $24.1 million, or $0.44 EPS. This compares with prior year period net income of $384.5 million, or $6.86 EPS, which included special item after-tax net charges of $20.2 million, or $0.36 EPS. Excluding special items, adjusted net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $463.3 million, or $8.41 EPS. This compares with adjusted net income of $404.7 million, or $7.22 adjusted EPS, in the prior year period.

​

Sales increased 12.9% to $3,562.4 million in the nine months ended September 30, 2026 primarily reflecting a 10.9% increase in organic sales, a 1.2% benefit from acquisitions, and 0.8% favorable foreign exchange. Operating income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $618.2 million, or 17.4% of sales. This compares with operating income of $533.7 million, or 16.9% of sales, in the prior year period. Excluding special items, adjusted operating income was $633.1 million, or 17.8% of sales, as compared with $549.1 million, or 17.4% of sales, in the prior year period.

​

Dividend

​

The Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.87 per share, or $3.48 per share on an annualized basis, a 10.1% increase from the prior quarterly cash dividend. The declared quarterly cash dividend of $0.87 per share is payable January 15, 2027 to shareholders of record as of December 31, 2026.

About Lincoln Electric

​

Lincoln Electric is a high-performance industrial machinery and technology leader who helps customers manufacture and maintain vital equipment and infrastructure. Lincoln Electric’s innovative solutions enable higher quality and productivity across a variety of processes including welding, cutting, brazing, machining, process automation and field repair. The Company leverages proprietary technologies and expertise in materials science, power electronics, automation and intelligent software to help customers build better and achieve resilience in their operations. Headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio, Lincoln Electric is the essential ‘Linc’ that keeps the economy running. The Company operates 71 manufacturing and automation facilities across 20 countries and serves customers in over 160 countries. For more information about Lincoln Electric and its products and services, visit the Company’s website at https://www.lincolnelectric.com.

Adjusted operating income, adjusted net income, adjusted EBIT, adjusted effective tax rate, adjusted diluted earnings per share (“adjusted EPS”), Organic sales, Free cash flow, Cash conversion, adjusted net operating profit after taxes and adjusted return on invested capital (“adjusted ROIC”) are non-GAAP financial measures. Management uses non-GAAP measures to assess the Company's operating performance by excluding certain disclosed special items that management believes are not representative of the Company's core business. Management believes that excluding these special items enables them to make better period-over-period comparisons and benchmark the Company's operational performance against other companies in its industry more meaningfully. Furthermore, management believes that non-GAAP financial measures provide investors with meaningful information that provides a more complete understanding of Company operating results and enables investors to analyze financial and business trends more thoroughly. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be viewed in isolation, are not a substitute for GAAP measures and have limitations including, but not limited to, their usefulness as comparative measures as other companies may define their non-GAAP measures differently.
