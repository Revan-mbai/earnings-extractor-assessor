HBT FINANCIAL, INC. ANNOUNCES
THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

Third Quarter Highlights
•Net income of $31.4 million, or $0.86 per diluted share; return on average assets (“ROAA”) of 1.85%; return on average stockholders' equity (“ROAE”) of 16.17%; and return on average tangible common equity (“ROATCE”)(1) of 19.27%
•Adjusted net income(1) of $31.4 million, or $0.86 per diluted share; adjusted ROAA(1) of 1.85%; adjusted ROAE(1) of 16.18%; and adjusted ROATCE(1) of 19.29%
•Asset quality remains strong, with nonperforming assets to total assets of 0.16% and net recoveries to average loans of 0.04%, on an annualized basis
•Net interest margin increased 3 basis points to 4.35%, and net interest margin (tax-equivalent basis)(1) increased 2 basis points to 4.40%
Bloomington, IL, October 26, 2026 – HBT Financial, Inc. (NASDAQ: HBT) (the “Company”, “HBT Financial” or “HBT”), the holding company for Heartland Bank and Trust Company, today reported net income of $31.4 million, or $0.86 diluted earnings per share, for the third quarter of 2026. This compares to net income of $27.8 million, or $0.76 diluted earnings per share, for the second quarter of 2026, and net income of $19.8 million, or $0.63 diluted earnings per share, for the third quarter of 2025.
J. Lance Carter, President and Chief Executive Officer of HBT Financial, said, “Our third quarter results were very strong with adjusted net income(1) of $31.4 million, or $0.86 per diluted share, an increase of 10.1% over the prior quarter. Profitability also remained strong with adjusted ROAA(1) of 1.85% and adjusted ROATCE(1) of 19.29%. Our net interest margin on a tax equivalent basis(1) was 4.40%, an increase of 2 basis points compared to the second quarter of 2026, as maturing fixed-rate loans repriced higher and securities cash flows were reinvested at higher rates, slightly offset by a higher cost of funds and lower loan accretion income. Noninterest income was above expectations, aided by wealth management, seasonally higher mortgage income, and a nonrecurring $0.4 million excise tax refund. Expenses were well controlled during the quarter, with an efficiency ratio on a tax equivalent basis(1) of 48.24%. Asset quality also remains exceptional, with nonperforming assets to total assets of 0.16%, and net recoveries to average loans of 0.04%, on an annualized basis, during the quarter. Finally, our balance sheet remains strong with all regulatory capital ratios increasing during the quarter, including a common equity tier 1 capital ratio of 12.94% and total capital to risk-weighted assets ratio of 16.48%.
Integration planning for the acquisition of Tri-County Financial Group, Inc. is progressing well, and we anticipate this transaction closing early in the first quarter of 2027, subject to their stockholder approval and regulatory approvals. We have been very impressed with their people and overall organization, and we are looking forward to a successful closing.”

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin
Net interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $70.3 million, an increase of 1.8% from $69.1 million for the second quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily attributable to higher yields on loans and higher average loan balances. A $0.5 million increase in loan fees and a $0.2 million increase in nonaccrual interest recoveries further contributed to the overall increase. Partially offsetting these increases were an $0.8 million decrease in acquired loan discount accretion, which totaled $1.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, and higher funding costs.
Relative to the third quarter of 2025, net interest income increased 40.7% from $50.0 million. The increase was primarily attributable to higher average interest-earning asset balances following the CNB merger and improved yields on debt securities and loans. Additionally, a $0.4 million increase in acquired loan discount accretion, a $0.4 million increase in loan fees, and a $0.3 million increase in nonaccrual interest recoveries further contributed to the improvement.

Net interest margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 4.35%, compared to 4.32% for the second quarter of 2026, while net interest margin (tax-equivalent basis)(1) for the third quarter of 2026 was 4.40%, compared to 4.38% for the second quarter of 2026. These increases were primarily attributable to improved yields on loans, which increased 5 basis points to 6.43%. These increases were partially offset by higher funding costs, which increased 4 basis points to 1.36%, driven primarily by increases in benchmark interest rates.
Relative to the third quarter of 2025, net interest margin increased 22 basis points from 4.13% and net interest margin (tax-equivalent basis)(1) increased 22 basis points from 4.18%. These increases were primarily attributable to improved yields on debt securities and loans as well as a more favorable interest-earning asset mix, which were partially offset by higher funding costs.

Noninterest Income
Noninterest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $14.2 million, a 20.2% increase from $11.8 million for the second quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily attributable to changes in the MSR fair value adjustment, with a $1.5 million positive MSR fair value adjustment included in the third quarter of 2026 results compared to an $0.8 million negative MSR fair value adjustment included in the second quarter of 2026 results. Additionally, a $0.4 million nonrecurring refund of stock repurchase excise tax as a result of a change in tax regulation included in other noninterest income contributed to the improvement.
Relative to the third quarter of 2025, noninterest income increased 44.6% from $9.8 million. The increase was primarily attributable to changes in the MSR fair value adjustment, with a $1.5 million positive MSR fair value adjustment included in the third quarter of 2026 results compared to a $0.5 million negative MSR fair value adjustment included in the third quarter of 2025 results. Additionally, an $0.8 million increase in wealth management fees, a $0.6 million increase in service charges on deposit accounts, and a $0.5 million increase in card income, all primarily driven by a larger customer base following the CNB merger, contributed to the increase.
Noninterest Expense
Noninterest expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $42.7 million, a 0.5% increase from the second quarter of 2026. Acquisition-related noninterest expenses totaled $1.0 million during the third quarter of 2026, compared to $0.3 million during the second quarter of 2026. Excluding acquisition-related expenses, noninterest expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $41.7 million, a $0.5 million decrease from the second quarter of 2026. The decrease was primarily attributable to a $1.1 million decrease in salaries expense, which was partially offset by a $0.4 million increase in occupancy expense, primarily due to planned building maintenance and upgrades, and a $0.3 million increase in employee benefits expense, driven by higher medical benefits costs.
Relative to the third quarter of 2025, noninterest expense increased 31.2% from $32.5 million. Excluding acquisition-related expenses, the $9.2 million increase in noninterest expense was primarily attributable to higher base costs following the CNB merger, including a $5.8 million increase in salaries and employee benefits expenses, which were also driven higher by annual merit increases and higher medical benefits costs, as well as increases in occupancy, data processing, and marketing expenses.

Loan Portfolio
Total loans outstanding, before allowance for credit losses, were $4.78 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $4.75 billion at June 30, 2026, and $3.40 billion at September 30, 2025. The $30.3 million increase from June 30, 2026 was primarily due to new fundings and draws on existing loans within the construction and land development category, partially offset by seasonal reductions of $8.8 million on grain elevator lines within the commercial and industrial category, as well as several payoffs due to refinance within the multifamily category.
Deposits
Total deposits were $5.75 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $5.76 billion at June 30, 2026, and $4.35 billion at September 30, 2025. The $5.6 million decrease from June 30, 2026 was primarily attributable to lower balances maintained in existing retail accounts and seasonally lower public funds balances, which were partially offset by an increase in business account balances. Additionally, $60.0 million of wealth management customer reciprocal deposits were moved on-balance sheet during the third quarter of 2026.
Asset Quality
Nonperforming assets totaled $10.8 million, or 0.16% of total assets, at September 30, 2026, compared with $9.9 million, or 0.15% of total assets, at June 30, 2026, and $8.6 million, or 0.17% of total assets, at September 30, 2025. The majority of the $0.9 million increase in nonperforming assets from June 30, 2026 was attributable to increases in nonaccrual loan balances in the one-to-four family residential category. Additionally, of the $10.1 million of nonperforming loans held as of September 30, 2026, $2.4 million were either wholly or partially guaranteed by the U.S. government.
The Company recorded a negative provision for credit losses of $0.6 million for the third quarter of 2026. The negative provision for credit losses primarily reflects a $1.9 million decrease in required reserves driven by changes within the portfolio, which included net recoveries of $0.5 million; a $0.6 million decrease in required reserves driven by changes in the economic forecast; a $0.1 million decrease in specific reserves; and a $2.0 million increase in required reserves resulting from changes in qualitative factors.
The Company had net recoveries of $0.5 million, or 0.04% of average loans on an annualized basis, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net recoveries of $0.1 million, or 0.01% of average loans on an annualized basis, for the second quarter of 2026, and net charge-offs of $0.1 million, or 0.02% of average loans on an annualized basis, for the third quarter of 2025.
The Company’s allowance for credit losses was 1.26% of total loans and 599% of nonperforming loans at September 30, 2026, compared with 1.27% of total loans and 666% of nonperforming loans at June 30, 2026. In addition, the allowance for credit losses on unfunded lending-related commitments totaled $6.6 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $6.6 million as of June 30, 2026.

About HBT Financial, Inc.
HBT Financial, Inc., headquartered in Bloomington, Illinois, is the holding company for Heartland Bank and Trust Company, and has banking roots that can be traced back to 1920. HBT Financial provides a comprehensive suite of financial products and services to consumers, businesses, and municipal entities throughout Illinois, eastern Iowa, and suburban St. Louis through 83 full-service branches. As of September 30, 2026, HBT Financial had total assets of $6.7 billion, total loans of $4.8 billion, and total deposits of $5.8 billion.
