Public Policy Holding Company, Inc. Announces Q3 2026 Financial Results
Continued Organic Growth and Expansion Through M&A; 2026 Guidance Reaffirmed
        
•Nine-Month 2026 YoY revenue growth of 15.2% with organic revenue growth of 4.7%
•Completed two acquisitions during the quarter, with one more acquisition concluding post-period
•Net Debt as of September 30, 2026 of $21.4 million; post-period announced expansion of credit facility
•Full Year 2026 guidance reaffirmed
WASHINGTON, DC – November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Public Policy Holding Company, Inc. ("PPHC," "Company," "Group") (Nasdaq: PPHC and AIM: PPHC.L), a leading global strategic communications provider offering a comprehensive range of advisory services in the areas of Government Relations, Corporate Communications, and Public Affairs, today reported unaudited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 ("Q3 2026") and for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 ("9M 2026").
Q3 2026 Financial Highlights
•Revenue increased 13.2% over Q3 2025 to $55.2 million
•Organic revenue growth of 5.1% over Q3 2025
•GAAP net income of $0.2 million, an improvement compared to the net loss of $7.4 million in Q3 2025, resulting from a $9.8 million non-cash bargain purchase gain on this quarter's acquisitions
•Adjusted EBITDA of $12.8 million, up 11.1% over Q3 2025, achieved at a 23.2% margin, reflecting incremental public company costs incurred following January 2026 U.S. IPO, and, to a lesser extent, a shift in business mix
•Adjusted Net Income of $10.7 million, up 8.7% over Q3 2025
•GAAP basic and diluted loss per share of $0.01, an improvement as compared to a loss of $0.45 per share in Q3 2025. GAAP net income is resulting in a GAAP net loss per share as a result of application of the two-class method, whereby the dividend declared on non-vested shares exceeded net income.
•Adjusted EPS, fully diluted of $0.33 compared to $0.36 in Q3 2025, reflecting the higher share count following January 2026 U.S. IPO
9M 2026 Financial Highlights
•Revenue increased 15.2% over 9M 2025 to $157.5 million
•Organic Revenue growth of 4.7% over 9M 2025
•GAAP Net Loss of $15.0 million compared to $23.8 million in 9M 2025
•Adjusted EBITDA of $36.2 million, up 9.9% over 9M 2025, achieved at a 23.0% margin
•Adjusted Net Income of $28.6 million, up 12.8% over 9M 2025
•GAAP Basic and diluted loss per share of $0.68 an improvement as compared to a loss of $1.51 per share in 9M 2025
•Adjusted EPS, fully diluted of $0.92 compared to $0.97 in 9M 2025, reflecting the higher share count following the January 2026 U.S. IPO
•Debt position of $39.8 million offset by cash of $18.4 million, resulted in a Net Debt position of $21.4 million compared to $38.5 million on September 30, 2025, due to proceeds of 2026 U.S. IPO and continued repayment of debt balances.

"PPHC delivered another strong quarter, with revenue increasing 13.2% and organic growth accelerating to 5.1%. Our Government Relations businesses performed particularly well, delivering double-digit revenue growth, substantial profit growth and robust margins. This performance demonstrates the strength of our underlying businesses and their ability to convert growing client demand into higher earnings.

"The performance reflects the benefits of our diversified platform, with particularly strong results in Government Relations, alongside continued execution of our growth strategy. "With a broad and diversified client base, lower year-on-year net debt and additional financial capacity, we remain focused on delivering consistent organic growth while selectively pursuing opportunities to strengthen the platform and create long-term value for shareholders."

"PPHC's nine-month performance reflects continued revenue growth, Adjusted EBITDA growth with margins stabilized and aligned with our prior guidance. We continue to manage the business back towards our 25% Adjusted EBITDA margin target as recently acquired businesses scale and as we absorb the first full year of U.S. public company costs. Alongside a focus on profitability, we also remain focused on investment in future growth as evidenced by the acquisition activity in Q3 and early Q4. With a strong balance sheet, and recently expanded debt facilities as announced on November 2, 2026, agreed with our partner Bank of America, recurring client relationships and a robust acquisition pipeline in North America, UK, and mainland Europe, we believe PPHC is well positioned to continue creating value for shareholders."
Reaffirms Financial Outlook
For full year 2026, PPHC is maintaining its guidance:
•Revenue in the range of $213 million to $216 million
•Adjusted EBITDA in the range of $48.5 million to $50.5 million, reflecting an adjusted margin between 22.5% and 23.5%
•Organic Revenue Growth of approximately 5%
This guidance includes the limited 2 months' contribution from the Reevemark acquisition (completed November 1, 2026) and continues to exclude the impact of any future acquisitions. The Company does not provide a reconciliation of forward-looking non-GAAP measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures because the reconciling items, including acquisition-related charges, share-based accounting charges and changes in the fair value of contingent consideration, cannot be reasonably predicted without unreasonable effort.
Operational Highlights
PPHC continued to execute its growth strategy through complementary acquisitions, senior hires and the development of new capabilities across the Group:
•Completed the acquisition of Tancredi on July 1, 2026, adding financial, corporate and litigation communications expertise as the first member of TrailRunner Group, the Group’s corporate and financial communications platform, and expanding international operations in London and Milan.
•Completed the acquisition of The Advocacy Partners on August 1, 2026, establishing PPHC’s government-relations capabilities in Florida and strengthening its state-level presence across key U.S. markets.

•Following the period end, completed the acquisition of Reevemark on November 1, 2026, adding specialist corporate, financial, litigation and crisis communications capabilities as the second member of the TrailRunner group.
•Strengthened senior talent with significant new hires in Government Relations, Corporate Communications, and Public Affairs.
•FTP and Seven Letter were named to PRNEWS’ Agency Elite Top 120, while professionals across the Group received individual recognition from Washingtonian, PRWeek and PRNEWS.
•Revenue diversification further enhanced with the top 10 Group clients representing 7.7% of revenue in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 9.2%). Revenue mix by segment also diversified further, with the Corporate Communications & Public Affairs segment, the Group's second-largest reporting segment, growing to represent 35.5% of total revenue in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 33.8%).
•PPHC's client base has grown to approximately 1,700 clients served in the last twelve months, including representations of approximately half of the Fortune 100 and many more via trade associations, reflecting continued high retention and new-business generation.
•The Group ended 9M 2026 with 484 employees (9M 2025: 451).
2026 Segment Results
•Government Relations Consulting revenue grew at 12.0% for 9M 2026, as compared to 9M 2025, as a consequence of continued organic growth of 6.7% in tandem with the acquisitions of Pine Cove Strategies, LLC ("Pine Cove") (completed July 11, 2025), WPI (completed April 1, 2026), and TAP (completed August 1, 2026). The margin of Segment Adjusted pre-bonus EBITDA increased by 3.0 percentage points to 47.2%, reflecting the consistent pricing of retainer contracts both at U.S. Federal and State level.
•Corporate Communications & Public Affairs Consulting revenue increased by 21.1% for 9M 2026, as compared to 9M 2025, driven by the impact of the acquisitions of TrailRunner International, LLC ("TrailRunner") (completed April 1, 2025), WPI (completed April 1, 2026), and Tancredi (completed July 1, 2026), while partially offset by a 0.9% organic revenue decline. The margin of Segment Adjusted pre-bonus EBITDA decreased by 2.5pts to 25.1% in 9M 2026, reflecting the inclusion of acquired revenues, representing operating margins that are lower than the Group's average, and reduced project volumes.
•Compliance and Insights Services continued its strong topline growth at 14.0% for 9M 2026, as compared to 9M 2025 (reported and organic) as a result of high renewal rates, price increases, and new client wins, reflective of a unique and high value-added offering. The margin of Segment Adjusted pre-bonus EBITDA was high at 48.9%, with the decline versus prior-year period primarily reflecting the Company's cost allocation structure for this segment rather than a change in its underlying profitability. The margin continues to benefit from strong pricing on subscription-based contracts and the increased use of technology in servicing clients.

About PPHC
Incorporated in 2014, PPHC is a global strategic communications platform that supports clients in enhancing and defending their reputations, advancing policy objectives, managing regulatory risk, and engaging with federal and state-level policymakers, stakeholders, media, and the public.
Engaged by approximately 1,700 clients over the last twelve months, including companies, trade associations and non-governmental organizations, PPHC is active in all major sectors of the economy, including healthcare and pharmaceuticals, financial services, energy, technology, telecoms and transportation.
With operations across the United States and internationally, PPHC's services include government relations, public affairs and corporate communications, research and analytics, digital advocacy campaigning, and compliance support. The Company's shares are admitted to trading on the Nasdaq Global Market and on AIM, a market operated by the London Stock Exchange, under the ticker symbol "PPHC".
For more information, visit www.pphcompany.com.

Cash Flow
PPHC's GAAP Cash Flow statement has certain acquisition-related payments included in the Cash provided by (used in) Operating Activities and in the Cash provided by Financing Activities, as a consequence of certain acquisition payments being made subject to continued employment.
The impact of acquisition-related payments on Cash provided by (used in) Operating Activities was especially pronounced in this year's nine months period. As a consequence, Cash provided by (used in) Operating Activities reduced from $10.0 million to $(16.5) million.
Consequently, in addition to our GAAP statement of cash flows, we use a non‑GAAP liquidity measure, Adjusted Free Cash Flow, to evaluate our cash generation. Adjusted Free Cash Flow should be viewed as supplemental to, and not a substitute for, GAAP net cash provided by (used in) operating activities and total changes in cash and cash equivalents.
In general, the generation of Adjusted Free Cash Flow tends to be weighted towards the second half of the year, as a consequence of the payment of annual bonuses in the first half of the year.
The Group recorded Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $14.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to $21.1 million in 2025. The decrease of $6.5 million in Adjusted Free Cash Flow is attributable to the Company's increased use of cash for working capital, stemming from higher receivables and the absorption of the working capital balances of our Q3 acquisitions. In 9M 2026, contract and unbilled receivables increased by $11.6 million in conjunction with accounts payable and accrued expenses decreasing by $2.9 million, totaling a $14.5 million change; while in 9M 2025, it was less pronounced at $8.0 million ($8.3 million increase in contract and unbilled receivables and $0.3 million increase in accounts payable and accrued expenses). The result was an increased use of cash for working capital of $6.5 million in 9M 2026 compared to 9M 2025.

Earnout obligations
As part of the typical structure applied for the acquisitions completed post-London IPO, the Group committed to certain contingent earnout payments. These earnout payments are based on a profit-driven formula and if the acquired company realizes profit growth after the date of completion. Payments are typically made in a mix of cash and shares. In turn, each of these components of earnout payments may be subject to further vesting requirements and employment conditions, which keeps the recipients financially committed to the Group.
In relation to these earnout payments, the Group has liabilities recorded of $32.5 million on its balance sheet, spread across the ‘Contingent Consideration’ and ‘Other Liabilities’ line items. This number reflects not only the estimated foreseen nominal payments, but also discount factors and fair value estimates. The liabilities accrued under 'Contingent Consideration' relate to regular M&A payments, while the liabilities accrued under "Other Liabilities" relate to those M&A payments that have 'continued employment' requirements and are therefore subject to 'clawback' provisions.
In nominal terms, over the period 2026 Q4 to 2031, based on expected performance of each of the acquired companies, management anticipates having to make earnout payments of $80.2 million(1), of which $43.8 million will be payable in cash and the remainder in shares.
The maximum earnout liability over that same period, which would only be reached if each acquisition meets very aggressive profit growth targets, would be $194.5 million, of which $111.6 million will be payable in cash and the remainder in shares. Generally, in order for an acquisition to reach maximum earnout payments, it would need to grow its profit by 25-30% annually during the entire earnout period.
Revisions to these expectations, relative to those reported in prior periods, are attributable to the execution of actual earnout payments, to the passage of time, as well as to modifications in the financial forecasts of the acquired companies.

Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Net Income are more useful performance indicators than the reported Net Income. The following elements distinguish our Adjusted Net Income from our Reported Net Income:
(1)Share-based accounting charge: As mentioned in all prior filings and annual reports, shares issued to employee shareholders at the time of the 2021 London IPO are subject to a vesting schedule. In addition, their employment agreements contain certain provisions which enable cash derived from the sale of shares at the time of the 2021 London IPO to be clawed back and forfeited on certain events of termination of employment. These items create a non-cash share-based accounting charge in accordance with guidance under U.S. GAAP, Accounting Standards Codification, 718- 10-S99-2, "Compensation-Stock Compensation". Based on the value of the Company at the time of
13






admission ($197 million) and the pre-admission employee shares sold in 2021, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, the non-cash charges are $7.2 million and $21.9 million (2025: $7.4 million and $22.2 million), respectively. This non-cash share-based charge has no impact on tax, nor share count or Company operations.
(2)Post-combination compensation charge: In the acquisitions that have been completed since the London IPO in 2021, the Group makes payments in cash and shares. In order to protect the interests of the Group, the shares issued as part of these transactions were made subject to vesting schedules. To a similar degree, also the cash paid as part of these transactions can be clawed back and forfeited on certain events of termination of employment.

The addition of these provisions to purchase price paid creates a post-combination compensation charge in accordance with accounting guidance under U.S. GAAP, Accounting Standards Codification, ASC 805-10-55-25, "Business Combinations - Contingent Payments". For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 the non-cash charges were $6.7 million and $13.7 million (2025: $4.0 million and $12.7 million), respectively. Again, this is a non-cash charge and has no impact on either tax or Company operations.
(3)LTIP charges. In 2022 the Group issued the first stock-based compensation units under the Public Policy Holding Company, Inc. 2021 Omnibus Incentive Plan. This plan was introduced at the time of the 2021 London IPO and allows the Group to issue up to a certain number of stock-related units (e.g. options, restricted stock). The charges relating to these issuances were $2.8 million and $4.8 million in the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (2025: $2.0 million and $4.6 million), respectively, and those were computed using the Black Scholes method.
(4)Amortization of intangibles: The non-cash amortization charge of $1.9 million and $5.0 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (2025: $1.6 million and $4.6 million), respectively, relates to the amortization of customer relationships, developed technology, and non-compete agreements per ASC 805.
(5)Bargain purchase: As laid out in point 2, because a significant part of the purchase price of our acquisitions is tied to continued employment, this part has been accounted for as post-combination compensation in the Group’s Consolidated Statements of Operations. As a consequence, for certain acquisitions, the remaining book purchase price is lower than the tax purchase price. The reason for the bargain purchase gain is tied directly to the tax purchase price significantly exceeding the book purchase price and is not a reflection of a true bargain purchase of the actual intangible and tangible assets of these acquisitions. The income recorded relating to the bargain purchase was $9.8 million and $10.7 million in the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (2025: zero and zero), respectively.
(6)Change in Contingent Consideration: The contingent consideration liability recorded as part of the acquisitions is adjusted at each reporting period for the change in the estimated fair value of that liability. The fair value changes over time based on management assumptions, the passage of time, payments made, and other external inputs, such as discount rates and volatility. The change in the estimated fair value of the contingent consideration is recorded as a non-operating expense of $1.7 million and $8.8 million in the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (2025: $2.3 million and $4.9 million), respectively.
(7)M&A expenses: since Q2 2025 reporting, the Group has been excluding M&A expenses from the Adjusted EBITDA. Reflecting our selective M&A strategy, M&A-related costs are highly variable across periods and may not occur in any given period. Expenses typically consist of M&A advisory fees, debt origination costs, and transaction related taxes.
