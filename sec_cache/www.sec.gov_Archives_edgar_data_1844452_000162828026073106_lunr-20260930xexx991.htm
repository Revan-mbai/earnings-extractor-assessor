Intuitive Machines Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results; Reports Record Financials Across The Board - Revenue, Profitability, Positive Cash Flow, and Backlog


Houston, TX, November 12, 2026 -- Intuitive Machines, Inc. (Nasdaq: LUNR, “Intuitive Machines,” or the “Company”),
leading space technology and infrastructure services company, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Intuitive Machines CEO Steve Altemus said, “In the third quarter, we delivered record financial performance across the board. These results are an important proof point in our transformation from a company that advanced the state of lunar exploration into a next-generation space prime developing and integrating the technology, infrastructure and services that enable customers to achieve their differentiated missions across space.”

Highlights

•Awarded as a vendor for NASA’s $10 billion (+$5 billion option) IDIQ contract in Q4 for its next-generation Commercial Lunar Payload Services 2 (CLPS 2) contract designed to support more capable cargo missions at greater frequency, advancing Artemis and Moon Base infrastructure requirements, including transportation, mobility, power, habitats, and logistics

•Booked $450 million of new awards and Goonhilly acquired backlog driven primarily by Accelerated Missile Defense Tranche 3 ("AMDT3") constellation, NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory EAGLE-VSWIR, and Hartford, two IM 300™ series platforms for a National Security Space application

•Contracted with NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) for FALCON-RADAR in Q4, which is part of the Fleet for the Atmospheric Linking Commercial Observations with NASA, an Earth observation mission that will study clouds and convection from space

•Accelerated plans for Near Space Network cislunar constellation, Altus-2 through Altus-5 to deploy together in 2028 on a NASA provided launch vehicle

•Closed Goonhilly Earth Station and COMSAT acquisition to expand our space‑to‑ground data services network configured to support missions across LEO, MEO, GEO, cislunar, and deep space environments

Financial Highlights

•Achieved record quarterly revenue of $269 million driven by execution across satellite production, lunar delivery, lunar data services, and ground communication networks

•Converted record revenue to record gross margin of $69 million or 26% driven by increased contribution from our satellite production business, higher volume, and net favorable one-time program adjustments, which also includes EAC and other cost adjustments

•Reported record quarterly cash from operations of positive $66 million with capital expenditures of $33 million, resulting in positive free cash flow of $33 million

•Ended Q3 with record backlog of $1.9 billion, an increase of $1.7 billion from year-end 2025 as Intuitive Machines booked record levels of diverse awards throughout the year across civil, commercial, and national security space customers

Mr. Altemus continued, “As we look to turn the corner into 2027, we remain laser focused on IM-3 along with our first Altus-1 lunar satellite, which remains on track for a planned launch in the March/April timeframe. In addition, our emphasis will be to continue to scale execution and accelerate growth. We expect to convert contracted backlog into revenue through repeatable production, operational excellence, and reliable customer delivery.”

Outlook

•Full-year 2026 revenue of $920 - $950 million

•Full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA positive

Key Business Metrics and Non-GAAP Financial Measures

In addition to the GAAP financial measures set forth in this press release, the Company has included certain financial measures that have not been prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”) and constitute “non-GAAP financial measures” as defined by the SEC. This includes adjusted EBITDA (“Adjusted EBITDA”), Free Cash Flow, and Backlog.

Adjusted EBITDA is a key performance measure that our management team uses to assess the Company’s operating performance and is calculated as net income (loss) excluding results from non-operating sources including interest income or interest expense from cash deposits, loans, or investments, transaction and integration costs related to acquisitions, gain on extinguishing of debt, non-recurring legal expenses, share-based compensation, change in fair value instruments, gain or loss on issuance of securities, depreciation, impairment of property and equipment, and provision for income taxes. Intuitive Machines has included Adjusted EBITDA because we believe it is helpful in highlighting trends in the Company’s operating results and because it is frequently used by analysts, investors, and other interested parties to evaluate companies in our industry.

Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical measure, and investors should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s results as reported under GAAP. Other companies, including companies in Intuitive Machines’ industry, may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently, which reduces its usefulness as a comparative measure. Because of these limitations, you should consider Adjusted EBITDA alongside other financial performance measures, including various cash flow metrics, net income (loss) and our other GAAP results. A reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure is included below under the heading “Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measure.”

We define free cash flow as net cash (used in) provided by operating activities less purchases of property and equipment. We believe that free cash flow is a meaningful indicator of liquidity that provides information to management and investors about the amount of cash generated from operations that, after purchases of property and equipment, can be used for strategic initiatives, including continuous investment in our business and strengthening our balance sheet. Free Cash Flow has limitations as a liquidity measure, and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our cash flows as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are: Free Cash Flow is not a measure calculated in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP; Free Cash Flow may not be comparable to similarly titled metrics of other companies due to differences among methods of calculation; and Free Cash Flow may be affected in the near to medium term by the timing of capital investments, fluctuations in our growth and the effect of such fluctuations on working capital and changes in our cash conversion cycle. A reconciliation of Free Cash Flow to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure is included below under the heading “Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measure.”

The Company has also included contracted backlog, which is defined as the total estimate of the revenue the Company expects to realize in the future as a result of performing work on awarded contracts, less the amount of revenue the Company has previously recognized. Intuitive Machines monitors its backlog because we believe it is a forward-looking indicator of potential sales which can be helpful to investors in evaluating the performance of its business and identifying trends over time.

About Intuitive Machines

Intuitive Machines is a next-generation space infrastructure company delivering integrated capabilities across spacecraft manufacturing, communications, networks, mission operations, and ground infrastructure to build, connect, and operate systems across Earth orbit, cislunar space, and deep space. Serving commercial, civil, and national security customers, Intuitive Machines is focused on enabling resilient, scalable infrastructure for sustained operations in space.

Backlog increased by approximately $1.65 billion as of September 30, 2026, compared to December 31, 2025. This increase includes $612.8 million of acquired backlog from the January 2026 Lanteris acquisition and $31.7 million from the August 2026 Goonhilly acquisition, as well as $1.77 billion in new awards. The new awards were primarily driven by a multi-satellite program supporting three commercial satellites, Space Development Agency Proliferated Warfighter Space Architecture tranche 3 tracking layer, Accelerated Missile Defense Tranche 3 ("AMDT3") constellation, NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory Explorer for Artemis Geology, Lunar, and Earth – Visible to Shortwave Infrared (“EAGLE-VSWIR”), and Hartford, two IM 300™ series platforms for a National Security Space application. Additionally, we recognized new awards or expanded contract values for the IM-5 and IM-6 missions, the NSN contract, and various other contract awards. These increases were partially offset by performance on existing contracts that resulted in recognized revenue of $662.2 million, and several adjustments of $94.7 million primarily related to the descoping of the remaining Power Propulsion Element (“PPE”) contract as well as partial descoping of a rideshare contract associated with the IM-4 mission.
