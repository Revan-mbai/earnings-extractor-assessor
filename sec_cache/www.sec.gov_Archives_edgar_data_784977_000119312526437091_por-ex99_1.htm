Portland General Electric Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

•
Third-quarter adjusted EPS of $1.06 reflects the unfavorable impact of historically mild weather
•
Industrial customer demand grew 9% year over year, driven by continued growth from high-tech and data center customers
•
Revising 2026 adjusted earnings guidance to $3.18 to $3.28 per diluted share, due to the impact of unusually mild weather during the third quarter; long-term guidance remains intact

 

PORTLAND, Oregon -- Portland General Electric Company (NYSE: POR) today reported third quarter 2026 net income of $119 million, or $1.00 per diluted share, on a generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) basis. After adjusting for business transformation, optimization and acquisition expenses, third quarter 2026 non-GAAP net income was $125 million, or $1.06 per diluted share. This compares with third quarter 2025 GAAP net income of $103 million, or $0.94 per diluted share, and non-GAAP net income of $110 million, or $1.00 per diluted share.

 

“We continue to execute on our strategic priorities. Industrial demand grew 9% on a weather-adjusted basis year over year, and cooler summer weather reduced residential and small-commercial demand,” said Maria Pope, President and CEO. “Our October 1st go-live of the Extended Day-Ahead Market is reducing power-costs and energy market volatility, delivering an estimated $14 million in customer savings in 2027.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Earnings Compared to Third Quarter 2025 Earnings

On an adjusted basis, third quarter earnings reflected continued execution on cost management initiatives, regulatory recovery and strong industrial load growth, partially offset by the impact of the mildest third quarter in a decade. Unfavorable weather reduced customer demand, resulting in a $0.15 year-over-year EPS headwind in the third quarter. On a year-to-date basis, weather has resulted in an estimated $0.20 EPS headwind relative to normal weather.

Additional Company Updates

Corporate Structure / Holding Company Update

Following the OPUC's September 25th order, the company filed a request for clarification and changes to two conditions. The proposed structure is intended to enhance financial flexibility while maintaining strong customer protections and utility financial strength. PGE's growth strategy, capital investment plan and earnings outlook do not depend on holding company implementation. The company supports $78 million in customer rate credits and $5 million in other community commitments.

 

Extended Day-Ahead Market

 

PGE successfully launched participation in the Extended Day-Ahead Market (EDAM) on October 1st, enabling lower power costs and enhanced tools to manage energy market volatility. PGE expects participation to provide meaningful customer value, including approximately $14 million in estimated customer savings in 2027.

 

General Rate Case

PGE filed its 2027 general rate case with the OPUC in August. As proposed, the case would result in an approximately 4.8% overall increase relative to currently approved prices with residential customers seeing an approximate 3.9% increase. If approved, new rates would take effect July 1, 2027. The increase is expected to be partially offset by lower net variable power costs in 2027, which are addressed separately through the Annual Power Cost Update Tariff. Parties’ opening testimony in the case is expected in mid-December.

 

Resource Planning and Procurement

In October, PGE filed its 2026 Clean Energy Plan and Integrated Resource Plan with the OPUC, proposing two pathways to meet projected resource needs while maintaining reliability and advancing Oregon’s decarbonization goals. Additionally, the company signed build-transfer agreements for two new solar and battery projects: a combined 200 MW solar and 250 MW battery storage facility at Biglow, selected from the 2025 RFP final shortlist, with an anticipated investment of approximately $900 million (excluding AFUDC); and Salmonfly, a combined 199 MW solar and 199 MW battery storage facility being developed with the Confederated Tribes of Warm Springs, with an anticipated investment of approximately $853 million (excluding AFUDC). The project was executed outside the 2025 RFP and remains subject to OPUC process as detailed in PGE's request for a waiver to the competitive bidding rules.

Quarterly Dividend

As previously announced on October 23, 2026, the board of directors of Portland General Electric Company declared a quarterly common stock dividend of 55.125 cents per share. The dividend is payable on or before January 15, 2027, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 22, 2026.

About Portland General Electric Company

Portland General Electric (NYSE: POR) is an integrated energy company that generates, transmits and distributes electricity to nearly 960,000 customers serving an area of approximately 2 million Oregonians. Since 1889, Portland General Electric (PGE) has been powering economies, delivering safe, affordable and reliable electricity while working to transform energy systems to meet evolving customer needs. PGE continues to make progress towards emissions reduction targets, and customers have set the standard for prioritizing clean energy with the No. 1 voluntary renewable energy program in the country. PGE is ranked a top ten utility in the 2025 Forrester U.S. Customer Experience Index. In 2025, PGE employees and retirees volunteered over 18,300 hours to more than 400 nonprofits organizations. Through the PGE Foundation, along with corporate contributions and the employee matching gift program, more than $5 million was directed to charitable organizations supporting economic growth and community resilience across our service area. For information: portlandgeneral.com/news.
