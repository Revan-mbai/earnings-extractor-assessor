Invesco Reports Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

Invesco Announces Third Quarter Diluted EPS of $0.65; Adjusted Diluted EPS (1) of $0.72

Atlanta, October 27, 2026 -- Invesco Ltd. (NYSE: IVZ) today reported financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
•$35.1 billion of net long-term inflows for the quarter, primarily driven by ETFs and Index, QQQ, Multi-Asset/Other and China JV, with ending AUM of $2.5 trillion
•23.3% operating margin in Q3 2026; 40.7% adjusted operating margin - an increase of 340 basis points and 320 basis points, respectively, from the prior quarter (1)
•Continued balance sheet strength - reduced net debt by $393 million during the quarter and increased common share buybacks to $65 million or 2.1 million shares (2)
•Repurchased $1.5 billion of the company's outstanding Series A Preferred Stock held by MassMutual on October 26, 2026, funded through a $1.5 billion five-year floating-rate bank term loan

Update from Andrew Schlossberg, President and CEO


“We continue to execute against our strategic priorities and leverage our advantageous global market position to deliver for clients and shareholders. We generated 6% annualized organic growth, driven by $35 billion of net long-term inflows, alongside $47 billion of global liquidity net inflows. This marks our 13th consecutive quarter of net long-term inflows. On a sequential basis, net revenue increased 4% and operating expenses declined 1%, driving operating income growth of 13% and expanding operating margin by more than three percentage points to 40.7%.(1)
Strong operating performance is enhancing our financial flexibility and supporting continued progress in strengthening the balance sheet. We have repurchased an additional $1.5 billion of the preferred stock held by MassMutual, bringing total repurchases to $3 billion over the past 18 months and leaving $1 billion outstanding. This ultimately enables reduced leverage, increases our balance sheet flexibility, and enhances earnings available to common shareholders. Our increasingly scaled platform and the exceptional talent across the organization are delivering profitable growth and supporting long-term value creation for clients and shareholders."

Net Flows:
Net long-term inflows were $35.1 billion for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $45.1 billion in the second quarter.

Retail net long-term inflows were $41.7 billion while Institutional net long-term outflows were $6.6 billion. Net long-term flows by investment capability include net long-term inflows from ETFs and Index of $28.5 billion, QQQ of $6.7 billion, Multi-Asset/Other of $3.3 billion, and China JV of $1.5 billion, partially offset by net long-term outflows from Fundamental Fixed Income of $2.6 billion, Fundamental Equities of $1.9 billion and Private Markets of $0.4 billion. On a geographic basis, the EMEA, Americas, and Asia Pacific regions achieved net long-term inflows of $15.9 billion, $10.3 billion, and $8.9 billion, respectively.

U.S. GAAP Operating Results:

Third Quarter 2026 compared to Second Quarter 2026

Operating revenues and expenses: Operating revenues increased $47.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter. Investment management fees increased $50.4 million, primarily driven by higher average AUM, partially offset by a reduction in revenue due to the sale of the management agreements associated with Invesco's Canadian fund business to CI Global Asset Management (“Sale of the Canadian Management Agreements”) in the second quarter of 2026. Service and distribution fees increased $3.3 million. Performance fees decreased $1.7 million to $2.0 million. Other revenues decreased $4.4 million as a result of lower real estate transaction fees.

Operating expenses decreased $24.7 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter. Third-party distribution, service and advisory costs increased $16.6 million primarily due to higher average AUM which was partially offset by a reduction in expense following the Sale of the Canadian Management Agreements. Employee compensation expenses decreased $52.9 million, primarily reflecting lower deferred compensation expense, driven by a $43.7 million decrease in the mark-to-market adjustment on deferred compensation liabilities and a $9.7 million decrease related to the timing of expense recognition for retirement-eligible employees. General and administrative expenses increased $15.9 million, primarily due to a net benefit of $11.7 million recorded in the second quarter of 2026 arising from the Sale of the Canadian Management Agreements and the restructuring of the company’s Canadian operations.

Non-operating income and expenses: Equity in earnings of unconsolidated affiliates was $42.6 million, earned primarily from our China joint venture and private markets investments. Interest and dividend income was $9.8 million earned from cash and cash equivalents and seed capital investments. Other gains/(losses) were a net loss of $34.2 million, primarily driven by market value changes in deferred compensation and other investments. Other income/(expense) of consolidated investment products (CIP) was income of $29.5 million, primarily driven by net interest income which was partially offset by market losses on the underlying investments held by the funds.

The effective tax rate was 23.3% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 25.6% in the second quarter of 2026. The decrease was primarily due to the favorable impact of net income attributable to non-controlling interests in consolidated entities recognized during the third quarter of 2026, which was partially offset by the favorable discrete tax benefit recognized in the second quarter of 2026 related to the gain on the Sale of the Canadian Management Agreements.

Diluted earnings per common share: Diluted earnings per common share was $0.65 for the third quarter of 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 compared to Third Quarter 2025

Operating revenues and expenses: Operating revenues increased $232.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. Investment management fees increased $327.5 million, primarily driven by management fees for QQQ following its conversion to an open-end fund ETF in the fourth quarter of 2025 and higher average AUM. Service and distribution fees decreased $87.5 million primarily due to the elimination of QQQ's pass-through service revenues after its conversion. Foreign exchange rate changes decreased operating revenues by $7.2 million.

Operating expenses increased $67.2 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. Third-party distribution, service and advisory costs increased $85.4 million primarily due to higher third-party costs for QQQ and higher average AUM. Employee compensation expenses decreased $27.8 million primarily due to a $24.9 million decrease in the mark-to-market adjustment on deferred compensation liabilities and lower salaries of $13.1 million primarily due to the divestitures in the fourth quarter of 2025, partially offset by higher variable compensation costs. Marketing expenses increased $15.8 million, primarily due to advertising related to QQQ. Foreign exchange rate changes decreased operating expenses by $5.4 million.

The effective tax rate was 23.3% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to (2.8%) in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily due to the favorable resolution of a tax matter in the third quarter of 2025, including the benefit related to a reduction in the deferred income tax rate and the reversal of a reserve for uncertain tax positions.

Adjusted (1) Operating Results:

Third Quarter 2026 compared to Second Quarter 2026

Net revenues and adjusted operating expenses: Net revenues in the third quarter of 2026 increased $52.6 million compared to the second quarter primarily due to higher average AUM and higher performance fees earned by our China joint venture, partially offset by the impact of the Sale of the Canadian Management Agreements.

Adjusted operating expenses decreased $11.0 million compared to the second quarter.

Net revenues and adjusted operating expenses: Net revenues in the third quarter of 2026 increased $195.4 million compared to the third quarter of 2025 primarily driven by higher average AUM and the net revenues earned from QQQ.

Adjusted operating income increased $156.2 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted operating margin increased to 40.7% from 34.2%.

The effective tax rate on adjusted net income was 25.1% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 11.2% in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily due to the favorable resolution of a tax matter in the third quarter of 2025, including the reversal of a reserve for uncertain tax positions.

(1)    Revenue adjustments: The company calculates Net revenues by reducing Operating revenues to exclude fees that are passed through to external parties who perform functions on behalf of, and distribute, the company’s managed funds. The Net revenue presentation assists in identifying the revenue contribution generated by the company, removing distortions caused by the differing distribution channel fees and allowing for a fair comparison with U.S. peer investment managers and within Invesco’s own investment units. Additionally, management evaluates Net revenue yield on AUM, which is equal to Net revenues divided by Average AUM during the reporting period, as an indicator of the Net revenues we receive for each dollar of AUM we manage.
Investment management fees are adjusted by renewal commissions and certain administrative fees. Service and distribution fees are primarily adjusted by distribution fees passed through to broker dealers for certain share classes and pass through fund-related costs. Other revenues are primarily adjusted by transaction fees passed through to third parties.
(2)    Invesco Great Wall: The company reflects 100% of Invesco Great Wall in its Net revenues and Adjusted operating income (and by calculation, Adjusted operating margin). The company’s non-GAAP operating results reflect the economics of these holdings on a basis consistent with the underlying AUM and flows. Adjusted net income is reduced by the amount of earnings attributable to the noncontrolling interests.
(3)    CIP: The company believes that the CIP may impact a reader’s analysis of our underlying results of operations and could result in investor confusion or the production of information about the company by analysts or external credit rating agencies that is not reflective of the underlying results of operations and financial condition of the company. Accordingly, the company believes that it is appropriate to adjust Operating revenues and Operating income for the impact of CIP in calculating the respective Net revenues and Adjusted operating income (and by calculation, Adjusted operating margin).
(4)    Amortization of intangible assets: The company removes amortization related to acquired assets in arriving at Adjusted operating income, Adjusted operating margin and Adjusted diluted EPS, as this will aid comparability of our results period to period, and aid comparability with peer companies that may not have similar acquisition-related charges.
(5)     Market valuation changes related to deferred compensation plan liabilities: Certain deferred compensation plan awards provide a return to the employee linked to the appreciation (depreciation) of specified investments. The company economically hedges the exposure to market movements on these deferred compensation liabilities. Since these liabilities are economically hedged, the company believes it is useful to remove the market movements related to the deferred compensation plan liabilities from the calculation of Adjusted operating income (and by calculation, Adjusted operating margin) and to remove the net impact of the economic hedge from the calculation of Adjusted net income (and by calculation, Adjusted diluted EPS) to produce results that will be more comparable period to period.
(6)    Canadian sale and restructuring: In the second quarter of 2026, the company removed the net benefit arising from the Sale of the Canadian Management Agreements and the restructuring of the company’s Canadian operations. The company removed this benefit in arriving at Adjusted operating income, Adjusted operating margin, Adjusted net income, and Adjusted diluted EPS, as this will aid comparability of our results period to period and aid comparability with peer companies that may not have similar activities.
(7)    Operating margin is equal to Operating income divided by Operating revenues.
(8)    Adjusted operating margin is equal to Adjusted operating income divided by Net revenues.
(9)    Impact of deferred income tax rate change: In the third quarter of 2025, the company removed a non-cash income tax benefit of $39.0 million related to the revaluation of the deferred tax liabilities for indefinite-lived intangible assets as a result of a decrease in the tax rate. The company removed this discrete tax benefit in arriving at Adjusted net income and Adjusted diluted EPS as it does not have a cash flow impact and will aid comparability of our results period to period.
