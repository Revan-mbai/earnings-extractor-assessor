BRC Group Holdings, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Completes Sale of Remaining Equity Interest in GA Group

LOS ANGELES, November 5, 2026 – BRC Group Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: RILY) (“BRC Group” or the “Company”), a diversified holding company, today released its financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Highlights and Recent Developments
•Revenues: Year-to-date total revenues of $624.2 million, including Operating Revenues(1) of $617.9 million. Q3 revenues of $33.0 million, as Operating Revenues(1) of $161.3 million were offset by Investment Losses(2) of $128.3 million.
•Net Loss: Year-to-date net loss available to common shareholders of $(13.2) million, or $(0.40) per diluted share. Q3 net loss available to common shareholders of $(243.0) million, or $(6.04) per diluted share, primarily reflecting $(226.7) million of pre-tax losses on the Company’s investment in Babcock & Wilcox and a $14.6 million non-cash impairment of its investment in GA Group; the Company’s other securities positions generated modest net gains.
•Operating Adjusted EBITDA(4): Year-to-date Operating Adjusted EBITDA of $103.3 million. Q3 Operating Adjusted EBITDA was reduced by $17.4 million, reflecting the reversal of an unrealized gain recognized in Q2 on structured equity financings and a net markdown of the SpaceX carried interest. Adjusted EBITDA(3) of $104.3 million year-to-date, and $(219.1) million in Q3.
•Balance Sheet: Fully redeemed $238.1 million of senior notes year-to-date, including $142.1 million in September. Subsequent to quarter end, the GA Group sale further reduced Oaktree term-loan indebtedness.
•Strategic Actions: Sold its remaining interest in GA Group to affiliates of Oaktree, reducing debt and adding liquidity (November 3); restored 100% ownership of B. Riley Securities Holdings (October 22); and entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Sangoma Technologies to scale its Communications platform (September 28).

Bryant Riley, Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer, commented:
“Through the first nine months of the year, our operating businesses demonstrated the resilience of our diversified platform, despite third-quarter declines in the value of our investment portfolio. Over the past several months, we have taken key strategic actions to strengthen our core franchises and allocate capital toward our strongest near-term cash flow opportunities. Earlier this week, we completed the sale of our remaining interest in GA Group. This legacy business generated significant value for our shareholders over more than a decade, and its sale sharpens our strategic focus, reduces debt, and enhances liquidity, supporting our operating businesses and a long-term approach to our investments through market dislocations. Consistent with that focus, we agreed to acquire Sangoma, a business with predictable cash flows that will bring meaningful scale and recurring revenue to our Communications platform and is highly complementary to our existing operations, creating opportunities for operating synergies. We also restored full ownership of B. Riley Securities and continue to advance its integration with B. Riley Wealth.”

“The third quarter was a challenging period for small- and mid-cap issuers, as rising rates and wider credit spreads weighed on transaction activity and equity capital markets issuance concentrated in the largest issuers. Against that backdrop, B. Riley Securities and B. Riley Wealth remain focused on delivering for all of our clients and partners. Whether that is helping navigate dislocation, creating capital markets opportunities, or providing strategic advisory, our platform is built to support our clients across every cycle. Even as smaller average deal sizes tempered underwriting fee revenue in the quarter, we saw rising demand for our restructuring and advisory services, added two senior bankers, and expanded institutional research coverage to more than 430 companies. Pending Acquisition of Sangoma
On September 28, 2026, the Company entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Sangoma Technologies Corporation (TSX: STC; Nasdaq: SANG), a business communications solutions provider, for a total enterprise value of $203.7 million. Sangoma shareholders will receive cash and the Company’s common stock, and are expected to hold approximately 4% of the Company’s pro forma outstanding shares. The transaction is expected to be funded with $212.3 million of committed senior secured term loans at the Company’s telecommunications subsidiaries, which will replace their existing credit facilities ($71.5 million outstanding at September 30, 2026), resulting in $140.8 million of incremental debt at those subsidiaries. Total equity contributions are expected to be $54 million, comprising $38 million from the Company and $16 million from its telecommunications subsidiaries. The transaction is not subject to a financing condition and is expected to close no later than early 2027, subject to Sangoma shareholder, court and regulatory approvals and other customary conditions.

Sale of Remaining GA Group Interest
On November 3, 2026, the Company completed the sale of its remaining equity interest in Great American Holdings, LLC (“GA Group”) to affiliates of Oaktree Capital Management, L.P. (“Oaktree”). The Company received total cash consideration of $62.3 million, of which $51.7 million was used to repay term-loan indebtedness owed to Oaktree affiliates. Concurrently, the Company amended its Oaktree credit agreement to add an incremental $26.5 million term loan and to eliminate any future prepayment premium, in exchange for a $3.3 million transaction fee paid in kind. After giving effect to these transactions, outstanding Oaktree term loans were reduced to $43.7 million from $65.6 million, and the Company added $36.5 million of liquidity, net of the closing fee. In the third quarter, the Company recorded a $14.6 million non-cash impairment to reduce the carrying value of its investment in GA Group to the sale price. Unaudited pro forma financial information will be filed by amendment to the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K no later than November 9, 2026.

Change in Non-GAAP Measures Presentation
The Company has updated its Operating Adjusted EBITDA calculation with adjustments that management believes better bifurcate the results of its investment activities from those of its operating businesses. The updated calculation, applied consistently across all comparable periods, now excludes income and loss related to the Company’s equity investment in Great American Holdings, LLC, as well as the noncontrolling interest associated with investment gains and losses attributable to B. Riley Securities Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries.

About BRC Group Holdings, Inc.
BRC Group Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: RILY) is a diversified holding company with operations in financial services, communications, and retail, alongside investments in equity, debt, and venture capital. Its core financial services platform provides small-cap and middle-market companies with customized end-to-end solutions at every stage of the enterprise life cycle. Its investment banking business offers comprehensive services in capital markets, sales, trading, research, merchant banking, M&A, and restructuring. Its wealth management business provides financial planning services, including brokerage, investment management, insurance, and tax preparation. Its communications businesses provide consumer and business services including traditional, mobile, and cloud phone, internet and data, security, and email. Its consumer products and retail businesses provide mobile computing accessories and home furnishings. BRC Group deploys capital inside and outside its core financial services platform to generate shareholder value through opportunistic investments. For more information, please visit www.brcgh.com.

Note Regarding Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Certain of the information set forth herein, including Operating Revenues, Investment Gains (Losses), Adjusted EBITDA, Operating Adjusted EBITDA, Total Investments, and Net Debt, may be considered non-GAAP financial measures, which are measures that differ from the most directly comparable measures calculated and presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). BRC Group Holdings, Inc. believes these measures are useful to investors as follows: Operating Revenues and Investment Gains (Losses) separate revenues generated by the Company’s operating businesses from gains and losses on its investment portfolio; Adjusted EBITDA and Operating Adjusted EBITDA are performance measures that exclude items management does not consider indicative of ongoing results, with Operating Adjusted EBITDA further excluding investment-related gains and losses; and Total Investments and Net Debt provide a basis for evaluating the Company’s capital resources and leverage.

As used herein, each measure is calculated as follows: (i) Operating Revenues includes services and fees, interest income - loans, interest income - securities lending, fixed income spread, trading gains attributable to variable rate transaction spread, and sale of goods; (ii) Investment Gains (Losses) includes trading gains (losses), net and fair value adjustments on loans, less fixed income spread and trading gains attributable to variable rate transaction spread; (iii) Adjusted EBITDA excludes (loss) income from discontinued operations, net interest expense, provisions for or benefit from income taxes, depreciation, amortization, restructuring charge, gain or loss on extinguishment of debt, gain on bargain purchase, gain on sale and deconsolidation of businesses, gain on senior note exchange, impairment of goodwill, tradenames and equity method investments, share-based payments, and transaction related and other costs; (iv) Operating Adjusted EBITDA excludes the aforementioned adjustments for Adjusted EBITDA as well as trading gains (losses), net, net of fixed income and variable rate transaction spread, fair value adjustments on loans, realized and unrealized gains (losses) on investments net of variable rate transaction spread, gains (losses) on investments attributable to noncontrolling interest, and income (loss) related to the Company’s equity investment in Great American Holdings, LLC; (v) Total Investments includes securities and other investments owned net of (a) securities sold not yet purchased, at fair value and (b) noncontrolling interest related to investments from continuing operations, loans receivable, at fair value net of loan participations sold, equity investments, and other investments reported in prepaid and other assets; and (vi) Net Debt includes term loans, net, senior notes payable, net, revolving credit facility, and notes payable, net of (a) cash and cash equivalents, (b) restricted cash, (c) due from clearing brokers net of due to clearing brokers, and (d) Total Investments.

In addition, the Company’s management uses these non-GAAP financial measures along with the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures in evaluating the Company’s operating performance, management compensation, capital resources, and cash flow. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information presented in compliance with GAAP, and non-GAAP financial measures as reported by the Company may not be comparable to similarly titled amounts reported by other companies.
