Regis Corporation Reports Financial Results for the First Fiscal Quarter 2027

Q1 Consolidated Same-Store Sales Up 0.2%, Supercuts Up 2.0%

Generates Positive Cash from Operations and Repays Approximately $7.9 Million of Debt

MINNEAPOLIS, November 5, 2026 -- Regis Corporation (NasdaqGM: RGS), a leader in the haircare industry, today announced financial results for the first fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Susan Lintonsmith, Regis Corporation’s President and Chief Executive Officer, commented, “In the first fiscal quarter, we achieved operating income of $5.3 million, Adjusted EBITDA of $7.7 million, and generated positive cash from operations, reinforcing a solid foundation for improving performance across our business. Supercuts delivered a 2.0% increase in same-store sales, contributing to modest overall growth across the Regis portfolio. At the same time, declining guest traffic in our company-owned salons requires immediate attention. We are taking targeted actions to increase traffic across our brands and build a stronger business that delivers sustainable growth and long-term value for our shareholders.”
Kersten Zupfer, Regis Corporation’s Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, stated, “During the first quarter, we repaid approximately $7.9 million of debt and ended the quarter with $20.8 million in cash. We recently completed a comprehensive review of refinancing alternatives with active oversight from our Board, and working closely with our newest director, who is a significant shareholder. We evaluated a range of options including senior bank debt, private credit financing, preferred equity structures, and whole business securitization. Based on the feedback we received, we determined that refinancing now would not deliver sufficient value to shareholders, given the scheduled decline in the call premium on our existing debt in mid-2027. We expect to return to the debt markets early in calendar 2027. In the meantime, we expect to continue generating cash and reducing debt according to our scheduled repayment obligations, thereby strengthening our financial position ahead of further refinancing discussions.”

Revenue
Total consolidated revenue of $54.3 million in the first quarter of 2027 declined $4.7 million compared to the same period last year, driven primarily by lower non-margin franchise rental income and company-owned salon revenue.
Operating Income
Regis reported first quarter 2027 operating income of $5.3 million, a decline of $0.6 million compared to $5.9 million in the first quarter of 2026, primarily driven by lower royalties and fees, and a $2.5 million accrual related to a Department of Justice (DOJ) inquiry concerning a Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) loan obtained by Alline that had been forgiven prior to the Company's acquisition of Alline in December 2024. The decline in operating income was partially offset by a $2.1 million gain on the derecognition of benefit obligations and overall corporate cost saving measures.
Net Income
The Company reported first quarter 2027 net income of $0.5 million, or $0.18 per diluted share, compared to net income of $1.4 million, or $0.49 per diluted share, for the same period last year. The lower net income in the current year period was driven primarily by lower operating income, partially offset by reduced income tax expense. Additionally, net income for the prior year period included a $1.0 million gain related to the Alline earnout liability adjustment.
Adjusted EBITDA
First quarter adjusted EBITDA of $7.7 million declined $0.3 million compared to adjusted EBITDA of $8.0 million in the first quarter of 2026. The decline is primarily related to lower royalties, fees, and company-owned salon earnings, partially offset by reductions in general and administrative expenses.

Franchise Revenue
First quarter franchise revenue was $35.4 million, a $3.3 million, or 8.5%, decrease compared to the prior year quarter. Non-margin franchise rental income was the primary driver of the decline due to franchisees signing their own leases and fewer franchise salons in the current year.
Royalties were $13.6 million, a $0.4 million, or 2.9%, decrease for the first quarter period, versus the same period last year, due primarily to fewer franchise salons.
Franchise Adjusted EBITDA
First quarter franchise adjusted EBITDA of $6.1 million declined $0.3 million compared to the prior year quarter. The decline primarily related to lower royalties and non-cash fees, offset partially by decreased general and administrative expenses.

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

About Regis Corporation
Regis Corporation (NasdaqGM:RGS) is a leader in the haircare industry. As of September 30, 2026, the Company franchised or owned 3,678 locations. Regis’ franchised and corporate locations operate under concepts such as Supercuts®, SmartStyle®, Cost Cutters®, Roosters®, and First Choice Haircutters®. Non-GAAP Reconciliations:
This press release includes a presentation of net income excluding certain non-cash charges, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted franchise revenue, and adjusted diluted earnings per share which are non-GAAP measures. The non-GAAP measures are financial measures that do not reflect United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). We believe our presentation of the non-GAAP measures provides meaningful insight into our ongoing operating performance and a supplemental perspective of our results of operations. Presentation of the non-GAAP measures allows investors to review our core ongoing operating performance from the same perspective as management and the Board of Directors. These non-GAAP financial measures provide investors an enhanced understanding of our operations, facilitate investors’ analyses and comparisons of our current and past results of operations and provide insight into the prospects of our future performance. We also believe the non-GAAP measures are useful to investors because they provide supplemental information that research analysts frequently use to analyze financial performance.
Items impacting comparability are not defined terms within U.S. GAAP. Therefore, our non-GAAP financial information may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies. We determine the items to consider as "items impacting comparability" based on how management views our business, makes financial, operating and planning decisions, and evaluates the Company's ongoing performance.
The reconciliation of U.S. GAAP net income to non-GAAP net income excluding certain non-cash charges is included in the release.
The following items have been excluded from our non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA results: stock-based compensation expense, gain on deferred compensation adjustment, gain on earn-out liability, Alline PPP loan matter, one-time professional fees and settlements, severance expense, the benefit from lease liability decreases in excess of previously impaired right of use asset, and lease termination fees.
We present adjusted revenue to provide a meaningful franchise adjusted EBITDA margin, which removes non-margin revenue from total revenue to arrive at an adjusted margin. Margin is a common metric used by investors, however, the majority of our revenue is offset by equal expense, so it does not contribute to our margin. We remove the non-margin revenue from this metric in order to show a meaningful margin rate.
The method we use to produce non-GAAP results is not in accordance with U.S. GAAP and may differ from methods used by other companies. These non-GAAP results should not be regarded as a substitute for corresponding U.S. GAAP measures but instead should be utilized as a supplemental measure of operating performance in evaluating our business. Non-GAAP measures do have limitations as they do not reflect certain items that may have a material impact upon our reported financial results. As such, these non-GAAP measures should be viewed in conjunction with our financial statements prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
