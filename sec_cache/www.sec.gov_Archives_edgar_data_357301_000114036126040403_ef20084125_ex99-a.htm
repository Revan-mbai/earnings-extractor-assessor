Glenville, New York – October 21, 2026
 
TrustCo Bank Corp NY (TrustCo, NASDAQ: TRST) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026 highlighted by a continued increase in net interest income and sustained loan and deposit growth across core lending and deposit categories. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, net interest income increased 9.1% year over year to $47.0 million. This was driven by the ongoing asset repricing across our loan portfolio at higher yields and effective execution of deposit growth and pricing strategies. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, net interest margin expanded to 2.95% from 2.79% in the prior year period. This resulted in third quarter 2026 net income of $17.3 million, or $1.02 diluted earnings per share, compared to net income of $16.3 million, or $0.86 diluted earnings per share, for the third quarter 2025; and contributed to net income of $50.6 million, or $2.91 diluted earnings per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net income of $45.6 million, or $2.41 diluted earnings per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Overview
 
Chairman, President, and CEO, Robert J. McCormick, said “Another quarter of solid financial performance is the product of TrustCo’s continued execution of tried and true banking principles that, quarter after quarter, continue to build positive momentum. Steady growth in both loans and deposits, coupled with favorable repricing of the Bank’s nearly $5.5 billion loan portfolio, have boosted net interest income to $47 million for the quarter, increased margin by 16 basis points, and driven return on average equity up 1.28% year over year, a 14% increase. Our management team has built a resilient balance sheet that can perform well in the current dynamic interest-rate environment, which efforts are supported by the entire organization’s focus on our relationship-based banking strategy. The ongoing repurchase of shares and regular payment of our recently-increased quarterly dividend work together to enhance shareholder value. With all of these factors coming together and trending favorably, we are optimistic about the Company’s ability to maintain positive momentum into 2027.”
 
Details
 
We have continued to see meaningful net income and net interest income improvement. The loan and investment portfolios of Trustco Bank (the “Bank”) continue to reprice upward as lower yielding assets mature and are replaced with higher rate loan originations and investment purchases, driving steady improvement in overall asset yields. We believe that this ongoing repricing reflects disciplined loan production aligned with current market conditions. Complementing this, the Bank maintains a strong liquidity position, driven by deposit growth while decreasing funding costs which underscores the Bank's disciplined relationship banking strategy and the value customers place on stability and service. We believe that these factors position the Bank to generate continued net income and net interest income growth in the coming quarters and deliver long-term value to shareholders. Net interest income was $47.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $3.9 million, or 9.1%, compared to the third quarter of 2025, driven by loan growth at higher interest rates and a decrease in interest expense. The net interest margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 2.95%, up 16 basis points from 2.79% in the third quarter of 2025. The yield on interest-earning assets increased to 4.35% in the third quarter of 2026, up 10 basis points from 4.25% in the third quarter of 2025. The cost of interest bearing liabilities decreased to 1.79% in the third quarter of 2026, down 11 basis points from 1.90% in the third quarter of 2025.
 
Average loans were up $238.1 million, or 4.6%, in the third quarter of 2026 over the same period in 2025. Average residential loans and Home Equity Credit Lines (HECLs), our primary lending focus, were up $177.3 million, or 4.0%, and $50.0 million, or 11.4%, respectively, in the third quarter of 2026 over the same period in 2025. Average commercial loans also increased $13.5 million, or 4.3%, in the third quarter of 2026 over the same period in 2025. Loan growth in the third quarter of 2026 remained steady, driven by continued strength in core relationship lending. Credit quality metrics were stable. Following this period of sustained growth, TrustCo remains confident in the quality of its loan portfolio amid broader market concerns. We believe that our continued focus on strong underwriting within our loan portfolio and conservative lending standards positions us to manage credit risk effectively in the current environment. The consistent growth in the loan portfolio will likely enhance net interest income in the quarters ahead. Average deposits were up $229.6 million, or 4.2%, for the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily as a result of an increase in time deposits, interest bearing checking accounts, and demand deposits. The Bank’s ongoing emphasis on relationship banking, combined with competitive product offerings and digital capabilities, has contributed to a broadening deposit base that supports ongoing loan growth and expansion.

During the third quarter of 2026, the Bank remained focused on capital deployment and allocation, guided by a disciplined framework, with a dividend increase, and share repurchases, these serve as key tools to enhance shareholder value. This reflects our confidence in the long-term strength of the franchise and our focus on capital optimization. During the third quarter, the Bank declared an increased quarterly cash dividend of $0.40 per share, or $1.60 per share on an annualized basis. The declared dividend represents an increase of 5.3% over the prior dividend amount which continues the Banks commitment in paying dividends for over a century. For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, TrustCo repurchased 500 thousand and 1.5 million shares, or 2.9% and 8.3%, respectively, of TrustCo’s outstanding common stock under its previously announced stock repurchase program, which authorizes TrustCo to repurchase up to two million shares, or 11.1%, of TrustCo’s outstanding common stock in 2026. We continue to believe that our approach ensures every dollar of capital is working to generate solid returns, strengthen customer relationships, and enhance shareholder value. As of September 30, 2026, our equity to asset ratio was 9.72%, compared to 10.90% as of September 30, 2025. Book value per share as of September 30, 2026 was $38.20, up 2.4% compared to $37.30 as of a year earlier.
 
Asset quality remains strong and has been consistent over the past twelve months. TrustCo recorded a provision for credit losses of $850 thousand in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $600 thousand compared to the same period in 2025. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the provision for credit losses was the result of a provision for credit losses on loans of $1.05 million due to loan growth and changes in forward looking economic assumptions, and a benefit for credit losses on unfunded commitments of $200 thousand due to the seasonal decrease in the loan pipeline. Accordingly, increases in the overall provision expense are being driven by loan growth and changes in economic forecasts rather than the deterioration in underlying credit performance. The ratio of allowance for credit losses on loans to total loans was 1.01% and 1.00% as of September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively. The allowance for credit losses on loans was $55.2 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $51.9 million as of September 30, 2025. Nonperforming loans (NPLs) were $21.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $18.5 million as of September 30, 2025. NPLs were 0.39% and 0.36% of total loans as of September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively. The coverage ratio, or allowance for credit losses on loans to NPLs, was 261.8% as of September 30, 2026, compared to 280.8% as of September 30, 2025. Nonperforming assets (NPAs) were $22.3 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $19.7 million as of September 30, 2025. Asset quality metrics remain stable and well covered by reserves, reflecting the Bank’s conservative underwriting standards.

About TrustCo Bank Corp NY
 
TrustCo Bank Corp NY is a $6.5 billion savings and loan holding company and through its subsidiary, Trustco Bank, operated 133 offices in New York, New Jersey, Vermont, Massachusetts, and Florida as of September 30, 2026.
 
In addition, the Bank’s Wealth Management Department offers a full range of investment services, retirement planning and trust and estate administration services. The common shares of TrustCo are traded on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol TRST.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures Reconciliation
 
Tangible equity as a percentage of tangible assets at period end is a non-GAAP financial measure derived from GAAP-based amounts. We calculate tangible equity and tangible assets by excluding the balance of intangible assets from total shareholders’ equity and total assets, respectively. We calculate tangible equity as a percentage of tangible assets at period end by dividing tangible equity by tangible assets at period end. We believe that this is consistent with the treatment by bank regulatory agencies, which exclude intangible assets from the calculation of risk-based capital ratios. Additionally, we believe that this measure is important to many investors in the marketplace who are interested in relative changes from period to period in equity and total assets, each exclusive of changes in intangible assets.
 
Adjusted efficiency ratio is a non-GAAP measure of expense control relative to revenue from net interest income and non-interest income. We calculate the efficiency ratio by dividing total non-interest expense as determined under GAAP by the sum of net interest income and total non-interest income as determined under GAAP. We calculate the adjusted efficiency ratio by dividing total non-interest expenses as determined under GAAP, excluding other real estate expense, net, by the sum of net interest income and total non-interest income as determined under GAAP, excluding net gains on equity securities. We believe that this provides a reasonable measure of primary banking expenses relative to primary banking revenue. Additionally, we believe this measure is important to investors looking for a measure of efficiency in our productivity measured by the amount of revenue generated for each dollar spent.
 
We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures provide information that is important to investors and that is useful in understanding our financial results. Our management internally assesses our performance based, in part, on these measures. However, these non-GAAP financial measures are supplemental and not a substitute for an analysis based on GAAP measures. As other companies may use different calculations for these measures, this presentation may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures reported by other companies.
