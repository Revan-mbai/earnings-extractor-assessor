Morningstar, Inc. Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results
CHICAGO, Oct. 28, 2026 - Morningstar, Inc. (Nasdaq: MORN) reported growth in revenue, operating and adjusted operating income, and margins in the third quarter.
"We continue to put important product enhancements in the hands of our clients while delivering profitable growth," said Morningstar CEO Kunal Kapoor. "This month, we launched Direct AI, which features agentic workflows backed by our trusted data, research, and analytics. As clients increasingly engage with our data within AI-driven workflows, we're also seeing strong adoption across our MCP offerings, with cumulative tool calls through the PitchBook Data Premium Connector surpassing 13 million in October, up from 1 million in July."
The Company's quarterly shareholder letter available at shareholders.morningstar.com provides more context on its third-quarter results and business performance.
Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Reported revenue increased 8.9% to $672.1 million compared to the prior-year period; organic revenue increased 6.8%. Excluding the impact of sunsetting certain products, organic revenue would have increased 7.8%.
•Reported operating income increased 31.0% to $167.4 million; adjusted operating income increased 26.1%.
•Diluted net income per share increased 41.0% to $3.06; adjusted diluted net income per share increased 36.5% to $3.48.
•Cash provided by operating activities decreased 2.6% to $190.7 million; free cash flow decreased 2.9% to $155.4 million, as higher cash earnings were more than offset by less favorable working capital movements compared with the prior-year period.
•Share repurchases totaled 486,573 shares for $100.0 million.
Year-To-Date Financial Highlights
•Reported revenue increased 9.7% to $2.0 billion compared to the prior-year period; organic revenue increased 7.0%. Excluding the impact of sunsetting certain products, organic revenue would have increased 8.3%.
•Reported operating income increased 31.9% to $483.9 million; adjusted operating income increased 26.8%.
•Diluted net income per share increased 41.6% to $8.61; adjusted diluted net income per share increased 35.9% to $9.76.




•Cash provided by operating activities increased 13.5% to $437.9 million; free cash flow increased 17.8% to $331.5 million.
•Share repurchases totaled 2,777,829 shares for $500.0 million.
Third-Quarter 2026 Results
Revenue increased 8.9% to $672.1 million on a reported basis and 6.8% on an organic basis versus the prior-year period. Morningstar Credit and Morningstar Direct Platform were the largest contributors to organic revenue growth. Excluding the impact of sunsetting Morningstar Office in Morningstar Wealth and the second-party opinions product in Morningstar Sustainalytics, organic revenue would have increased 7.8%.
Operating expense increased 3.0% to $504.7 million versus the prior-year period, primarily driven by:
•A $7.1 million increase in certain technology infrastructure costs largely driven by cloud costs associated with the migration from on-premise data centers and costs associated with AI initiatives.
•A $5.3 million increase in advertising and marketing expense, primarily driven by corporate brand activities and the launch of the Game On brand campaign.
These increases were partially offset by a $7.9 million decline in compensation costs. Lower compensation costs were primarily driven by a decline in bonus expense compared to the relatively high level in the prior-year period, partially offset by a $6.9 million increase in severance costs.
Third-quarter operating income increased 31.0% to $167.4 million. Adjusted operating income was $189.9 million, an increase of 26.1%. Third-quarter operating margin was 24.9%, compared with 20.7% in the prior-year period. Adjusted operating margin was 28.3% in the third quarter of 2026, versus 24.4% in the prior-year period. The acquisition of CRSP was accretive to adjusted operating margin in the quarter.
Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $115.5 million, or $3.06 per diluted share, compared with net income of $91.6 million, or $2.17 per diluted share, in the prior-year period, an increase of 41.0% on a per diluted share basis. Adjusted diluted net income per share increased 36.5% to $3.48 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $2.55 in the prior-year period.
The Company's effective tax rate decreased to 25.0% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 25.5% in the prior-year period.
Segment Highlights
Morningstar Direct Platform
Morningstar Direct Platform contributed $222.5 million to consolidated revenue and $11.4 million to consolidated revenue growth, with revenue increasing 5.4% compared to the prior-year period, or 6.2% on an organic basis. Higher revenue was primarily driven by Morningstar Data and Morningstar Direct. Morningstar Data organic revenue growth was driven in part by continued expansion with existing clients supported by new use cases, with strength in the managed investment data and

Morningstar Essentials products. Morningstar Direct growth reflected increased revenue per license and expansion with existing clients in reporting solutions. Direct licenses were relatively flat compared with the prior-year period.
Morningstar Direct Platform adjusted operating income increased 11.2% to $104.2 million, and adjusted operating margin increased 2.4 percentage points to 46.8%.
PitchBook
PitchBook contributed $175.6 million to consolidated revenue and $6.6 million to consolidated revenue growth, with revenue increasing 3.9% compared to the prior-year period, or 4.0% on an organic basis. The increase in revenue was primarily driven by strong growth across multiple core client segments in direct data, which continued to expand from a smaller revenue base. PitchBook platform growth slowed with particular softness in the corporate and venture capital client segments, which continued to experience higher churn. PitchBook licensed users grew 4.6% compared to the prior-year period, including the addition of new PitchBook Data Premium Connector licensed users in the quarter associated with a large renewal.
PitchBook adjusted operating income decreased 1.1% to $52.3 million, and adjusted operating margin decreased 1.5 percentage points to 29.8% primarily due to an increase in compensation and technology infrastructure costs. Higher compensation costs were largely driven by an increase in commissions related in part to several large direct data transactions and an increase in severance expense, while the increase in technology infrastructure costs was driven in part by AI initiatives.
Morningstar Credit
Morningstar Credit contributed $107.8 million to consolidated revenue and $16.7 million to consolidated revenue growth, with revenue increasing 18.3% compared to the prior-year period, or 19.1% on an organic basis, supported by robust issuance. Revenue grew across geographies and asset classes, with particular strength in corporates and US asset-backed and residential mortgage-backed securities ratings.
Morningstar Credit adjusted operating income increased 44.5% to $40.6 million, and adjusted operating margin increased 6.9 percentage points to 37.7%. The increase in adjusted operating income and margin reflected revenue growth, partially offset by compensation costs driven by higher salaries due to added headcount to support growth.
Morningstar Wealth
Morningstar Wealth contributed $59.4 million to consolidated revenue and negative $2.7 million to consolidated revenue growth, with revenue decreasing 4.3% compared to the prior-year period, or 6.0% on an organic basis. Excluding the impact of sunsetting Morningstar Office, organic revenue would have increased 2.1%, supported by growth in Investment Management partially offset by lower advertising sales.
Morningstar Wealth reported assets under management and advisement (AUMA) decreased 8.5% to $64.8 billion compared with the prior-year period, primarily due to the loss of an Asset Allocation Services client in Japan, which accounted for a negligible share of Investment Management revenue. Combined Morningstar Model Portfolios and International Wealth Platform AUMA increased 12.1% to $56.5 billion, largely driven by market appreciation and supported by positive net flows outside the US.
Morningstar Wealth adjusted operating income increased 138.7% to $7.4 million, and adjusted operating margin increased 7.5 percentage points to 12.5%.

Morningstar Retirement
Morningstar Retirement contributed $41.5 million to consolidated revenue and $7.3 million to consolidated revenue growth. Revenue increased 21.3% on a reported and organic basis. AUMA increased 12.1% to $333.9 billion compared with the prior-year period primarily due to market gains. Positive net flows, primarily driven by Advisor Managed Accounts, also contributed.
Morningstar Retirement adjusted operating income increased 40.2% to $23.7 million, and adjusted operating margin increased 7.7 percentage points to 57.1%.
Corporate and All Other
Revenue attributable to Corporate and All Other contributed $65.3 million to consolidated revenue and $15.4 million to consolidated revenue growth, with reported revenue increasing 30.9%, or 1.8% on an organic basis, compared to the prior-year period, driven by Morningstar Indexes. Organic revenue growth excludes revenue associated with the CRSP acquisition and foreign currency impact.
Morningstar Indexes revenue increased on a reported and organic basis, with reported growth driven primarily by the CRSP acquisition. Morningstar Sustainalytics revenue declined on a reported and organic basis primarily due to the sunsetting of the second-party opinions product.
The impact of Corporate and All Other on consolidated adjusted operating income was negative $38.3 million compared with negative $44.1 million in the prior-year period.
Balance Sheet and Capital Allocation
As of Sept. 30, 2026, the Company had cash, cash equivalents, and investments totaling $524.6 million and $1.7 billion of debt, compared with $528.7 million and $1.1 billion, respectively, as of Dec. 31, 2025.
Cash provided by operating activities decreased 2.6% to $190.7 million. Free cash flow decreased 2.9% to $155.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, as higher cash earnings were more than offset by less favorable working capital movements compared with the prior-year period.
During the quarter, the Company reduced its debt by $34.7 million, net, repurchased $100.0 million of its shares, and paid $18.7 million in dividends.

Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Measures with the Nearest Comparable GAAP Measures (Unaudited)
To supplement Morningstar’s condensed consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with US Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP), Morningstar uses the following measures considered as non-GAAP by the SEC, including:

•"Organic Revenue" is consolidated revenue before (1) acquisitions and divestitures, (2) adoption of new accounting standards or revisions to accounting practices (accounting changes), and (3) the effect of foreign currency translations.
•"Adjusted Operating Income (Loss)" is consolidated operating income (loss) excluding (1) intangible amortization expense, (2) the impact of merger, acquisition, and divestiture-related activity which, when applicable, may include certain non-recurring expenses such as pre-deal due diligence, transaction costs, contingent consideration, severance, and post-close integration costs (M&A-related expenses), and (3) certain other one-time, non-recurring items which management does not consider when evaluating ongoing performance (other non-recurring items).
•"Adjusted Operating Margin" is operating margin excluding (1) intangible amortization expense, (2) M&A-related expenses, and (3) other non-recurring items.
•"Adjusted Diluted Net Income Per Share" is consolidated diluted net income per share excluding (1) intangible amortization expense, (2) M&A-related expenses, (3) other non-recurring items, and (4) non-operating gains and losses.
•"Free Cash Flow" is cash provided by or used for operating activities less capital expenditures.
These non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies and should not be considered an alternative to any measure of performance promulgated under GAAP.
Morningstar presents organic revenue because the Company believes this non-GAAP measure helps investors better compare period-over-period results, and Morningstar's management team uses this measure to evaluate the performance of our business. Morningstar excludes revenue from acquired businesses from its organic revenue growth calculation for a period of 12 months after it completes the acquisition. For divestitures (including sale of assets), Morningstar excludes revenue in the prior-year period for which there is no comparable revenue in the current period.

Morningstar presents adjusted operating income (loss), adjusted operating margin, and adjusted diluted net income per share because Morningstar believes they better reflect period-over-period comparisons, and improve overall understanding of the underlying performance of the business absent the impact of intangible amortization expense, M&A-related expenses, and certain other one-time, non-recurring items.
In addition, Morningstar presents free cash flow as a supplemental disclosure to help investors better understand how much cash is available after making capital expenditures. Morningstar's management team uses free cash flow as a metric to evaluate the health of its business.
