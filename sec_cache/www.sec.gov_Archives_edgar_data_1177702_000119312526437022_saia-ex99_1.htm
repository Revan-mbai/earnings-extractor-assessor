Saia Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Announces $250 Million Stock Repurchase Authorization

 

JOHNS CREEK, GA – October 29, 2026 – Saia, Inc. (Nasdaq: SAIA) today reported third quarter 2026 financial results. Diluted earnings per share for the quarter were $3.41 compared to $3.22 in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding a 2025 net gain on real estate recorded in the third quarter of 2025, adjusted diluted earnings per share1 were $2.81 in the third quarter of 2025. Saia also announced that its Board
of Directors has authorized the repurchase of up to $250 million of Saia’s common stock.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Compared to Third Quarter 2025 Results

 

•
Revenue was a company record of $982.5 million, an increase of 17.0%
•
LTL tonnage per workday was a record, and an increase of 8.9%
•
LTL revenue per shipment, excluding fuel surcharge revenue, increased 5.8%
•
LTL shipments per workday increased 0.8%
•
Operating income was $122.3 million, an increase of 3.1% compared to $118.6 million in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 17.5% compared to adjusted operating income1 of $104.1 million in the prior-year period
•
Operating ratio of 87.6% compared to 85.9% in the third quarter of 2025, or 87.6% on an adjusted basis1 in the prior-year period

 

Saia President and CEO, Fritz Holzgrefe, said, “Our team delivered solid third quarter performance, which included record revenue and tonnage per workday. Revenue per shipment, excluding fuel surcharge, increased 5.8% compared with the third quarter of last year, reflecting continued progress in pricing and mix optimization. We continue to execute our long-term strategy to build a leading national LTL carrier. Investments in our network, people, equipment and technology are strengthening our customer value proposition and further positioning Saia to deliver sustained, profitable growth and long-term value for stockholders.”

 

Executive Vice President and CFO, Matt Batteh, said, “Our consistent execution and continued investment in the business are translating into measurable results, including market share gains, improved service and strong free cash flow generation. Year to date, we have generated more than $240 million in free cash flow1, providing the financial flexibility to support profitable growth while positioning us to return capital

Financial Position and Capital Expenditures

 

Saia ended the third quarter of 2026 with $202.5 million of cash on hand and $100.0 million of total debt, compared to $35.5 million of cash on hand and $219.2 million of total debt at September 30, 2025.

 

Net capital expenditures were $235.9 million during the first nine months of 2026, compared to $446.1 million in net capital expenditures during the first nine months of 2025. For full-year 2026, the Company expects that net capital expenditures will be approximately $350 million, subject to ongoing evaluation of market conditions.

 

Stock Repurchase Authorization

 

Saia today announced that its Board of Directors approved a stock repurchase program authorizing the Company to repurchase up to $250 million of its outstanding common stock. The program is effective immediately and permits shares of common stock to be repurchased from time to time at management’s discretion through a variety of methods, including a 10b5-1 trading plan, open market purchases, privately negotiated transactions, or other transactions permitted by applicable securities laws and regulations. The timing and amount of repurchases will depend on market conditions and other factors, including business conditions, alternative investment opportunities, funding considerations, and other matters considered relevant by management and the Board. The stock repurchase program has no expiration date, does not obligate the Company to acquire a specified number of shares and may be suspended, modified or terminated by the Company at any time, without prior notice.

1 Non-GAAP Financial Disclosure and Reconciliation:

From time to time we supplement the reporting of our financial information determined under generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”) with certain non-GAAP financial measures. These include “adjusted” total operating expenses, “adjusted” operating income, “adjusted” diluted earnings per share, free cash flow and “adjusted” operating ratio. The Company’s management believes that certain non-GAAP financial measures provide a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhance comparability of results with prior periods. The Company’s management believes that investors may use these non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate the Company’s financial performance without the impact of items that may obscure trends in the Company’s underlying performance. These disclosures should not be viewed as a substitute for financial measures determined in accordance with GAAP, nor are they necessarily comparable to non-GAAP performance measures that may be presented by other companies.

A gain from the sale of a terminal of $16.4 million and a loss on real estate impairment of $1.9 million was recorded during the third quarter of 2025. This resulted in a decrease in operating expenses and an increase in operating income of $14.5 million and an increase in diluted earnings per share of $0.41. The transactions increased diluted earnings per share by $0.54 on a pre-tax basis, partially offset by related income tax expense of $0.13. Additionally, this produced an improvement of 170 basis points in the operating ratio for the third quarter of 2025. The terminal sale occurred as the result of management’s efforts towards expanding door count by replacing a smaller facility with a larger facility better positioned to successfully support the Company’s overall strategy. The impairment loss came as a result of management's continued assessment of the recoverability of property and equipment.
