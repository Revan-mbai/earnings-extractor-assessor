Codexis Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

Reports revenue of $19.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, company reiterates full-year revenue guidance

Shared new advances in RNA therapeutic manufacturing at TIDES Europe

Entered into a $60 million term loan facility with Trinity Capital; initial tranche used to refinance current debt

REDWOOD CITY, Calif., November 12, 2026 — Codexis, Inc. (NASDAQ: CDXS), a leading provider of enzymatic solutions for efficient and scalable manufacturing of complex therapeutics, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

“I am pleased with our outstanding progress in 2026, through both innovation and technical achievements, that has garnered attention from leading innovators in the siRNA field,” stated Alison Moore, PhD, President and Chief Executive Officer of Codexis. “Last week we presented new data at the TIDES Europe annual meeting showing successful scale-up of the ECO Synthesis® Manufacturing Platform at 100-gram scale with high product quality, and including improved process performance with reduced cycle time. In addition, we significantly strengthened our balance sheet with an equity raise of approximately $25 million in net proceeds, completed in July. This was followed by a $60 million term loan agreement which we closed earlier this month, of which $42.5 million was used to refinance our existing debt. We have made significant progress in industrializing our technology while simultaneously increasing our revenue base, managing expenses, and improving the financial stability of our company.”

Third Quarter and Recent Business Highlights

 

  •  

In November, the company completed a $60 million senior secured loan agreement with Trinity Capital. The first tranche of $42.5 million was used to replace the existing agreement the company had with Innovatus Life Sciences Lending Fund I, LP. The remainder of the facility is available for future drawdown, subject to certain conditions.

 

  •  

In November, Codexis hosted key presentations at the 2026 TIDES Europe annual meeting. These presentations demonstrated successful production of 100 grams of high-quality oligonucleotide. The company also showed greater potency of stereo-defined siRNA as compared to chemically synthesized material. These presentations have continued to expand industry-wide interest in the ECO Synthesis Manufacturing Platform.

 

  •  

In October, Codexis announced a feasibility study with a pioneer company in the siRNA field, where Codexis will use its ECO Synthesis technology to synthesize stereo-defined fragments. This innovator uses a fragment synthesis and ligation strategy and is seeking a method to increase the purity of fragments above what can be attained using standard solid phase chemistry techniques.

 

  •  

The company successfully completed an equity financing in July that raised a total of approximately $25 million in net proceeds.

 

Upcoming Milestones

 

  •  

Advance its partnerships with drug innovators toward clinical stage manufacturing agreements.

 

  •  

Codexis expects to begin retrofit construction for its ECO GMP Manufacturing Center in the new year, with the facility being operational by the end of 2027. The purpose of this facility is to provide siRNA material for preclinical investigations and Phase 1 and 2 clinical trials.

 

  •  

Expand relationships with existing and future CDMO partners with a goal of commencing a strategic partner agreement.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

  •  

Total revenues were $19.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $8.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher product revenue, driven by the approval and launch of new products by our customers with higher margins compared to more established products.

 

  •  

Product gross margin was 74.5% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 64% in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in gross margin was largely due to a shift in sales toward more profitable products, and declines in less profitable, established products.

 

  •  

Research and Development expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $12.6 million compared to $13.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower employee-related costs, reduced spending on outside services and lower lab supplies, partially offset by higher allocable costs.

 

  •  

Selling, General & Administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $10.1 million compared to $11.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower employee-related costs associated with reduced headcount, lower stock-based compensation expenses, and lower allocable costs, partially offset by higher facility-related expenses.

 

  •  

The net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $8.1 million, or $0.08 per share, compared to a net loss of $19.6 million, or $0.22 per share, for the third quarter of 2025.

 

  •  

As of September 30, 2026, Codexis had $72.7 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments. During the third quarter, Codexis successfully completed an equity financing that raised a total of approximately $25 million in net proceeds. In addition, after the close of the third quarter Codexis entered into a $60 million term loan facility with a $42.5 million drawdown at closing, which was used to retire existing debt.

2026 Financial Revenue Guidance Reiterated, Gross Margin Estimate Increased

Codexis reiterated and updated its full-year 2026 financial guidance as follows:

 

  •  

Total revenues are expected to be in the range of $72 million to $76 million. Gross margin for the year 2026 is expected to be in the low 70% range.

 

  •  

Codexis expects that its existing cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments will be sufficient to fund its planned operations through the end of 2028. The financial guidance and cash runway projections include the expenses associated with the build out of the GMP production facility.

About Codexis

Codexis® is a leading provider of high-performance enzymatic solutions for efficient and scalable therapeutics manufacturing. The company is currently developing its proprietary ECO Synthesis® Manufacturing Platform, an aqueous enzymatic process that may offer a scalable alternative to traditional solid phase organic synthesis of oligonucleotide manufacturing. Initially, Codexis is applying its technology to address the rapidly growing demand for RNAi therapeutics. Codexis’ unique enzymes can drive improvements such as higher yields, increased purity, reduced energy usage and waste generation, stereoisomer control of siRNA, and improved overall capacity in manufacturing at substantially lower capital requirements, all of which may lead to greater efficiency and reduced costs. In addition, the company supplies high-performance enzymes through its BioCatalysis business that are used in the manufacture of multiple commercially available small molecule therapeutics. For additional information, please visit https://www.codexis.com.
